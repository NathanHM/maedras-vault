# Clergy and Worship  
Clerics and followers of [[Vel'Enar]] see their role as guides and mediators between life and death. They help people make peace with death, either through funerary rites or counselling the dying. They also celebrate births, viewing new life as the return of a soul to the material world after its time in another form. Rituals often take place at twilight, the time between day and night, symbolizing the transition between life and death. The changing seasons are also important to their worship, and many ceremonies are held at the equinoxes, when the balance between light and dark shifts.  
### Funerary Rites  
A typical rite involves placing the deceased on a bed of flowers and greenery, symbolizing the return to the earth, and lighting a candle to guide their soul into the next life.  
### Celebration of Life  
Followers often hold celebrations when new life enters the world, as they believe this is a sign of a soul returning from its journey in another realm.  
## [[High Lady Dawn]] (Funeral Rites)  
**Role**: As the overseer of funeral rites, [[High Lady Dawn]] guides souls from the world of the living to the world beyond. She embodies [[Vel'Enar]]'s principle of "Guide, Don't Judge," leading the departed with peace and respect for their journey.  
**Rituals**: Dawn holds twilight vigils, where lanterns are lit to symbolize the path to the next life. These rites are gentle and serene, often accompanied by songs that recount the individual's life journey.  
**Followers**: Those who find comfort in endings or have lost loved ones often seek her counsel, finding solace in her impartial perspective on death as a transition rather than a loss.  
## [[High Lady Dusk]] (Birth Rites)  
**Role**: [[High Lady Dusk]] is responsible for rites of birth, welcoming souls back into the cycle of life. She reflects the teaching "Honour the Journey," viewing each new life as a soul returning to the material world.  
**Rituals**: At dawn, infants are blessed with a drop of water mixed with soil to symbolize both growth and grounding. She tells families that each soul has returned for new experiences and learning.  
**Followers**: Families and expectant parents often turn to [[High Lady Dusk|Dusk]] for blessings, as she embodies the cycle's renewal and provides wisdom on the roles of growth and rebirth.  
## [[Lady Mid]] (Lambing)  
**Role**: [[Lady Mid]] oversees lambing and livestock birth, aligning with [[Vel'Enar]]'s teachings to "Embrace the Cycle." She ensures animals are brought into the world with reverence, viewing them as part of the natural balance.  
**Rituals**: At each lambing, [[Lady Mid|Mid]] recites verses of life's cyclic nature and ensures offerings to [[Vel'Enar]], thanking him for the vitality and nourishment these creatures bring. She believes they, too, contribute to the ongoing dance between life and death.  
**Followers**: Farmers and herders closely follow [[Lady Mid]]'s guidance, relying on her knowledge for successful lambing and fostering a respectful relationship with the land.  
## [[Lady Night]] (Harvest)  
**Role**: [[Lady Night]] manages the harvest rites, upholding "Respect the Balance" by gathering what nature provides without excess. She interprets [[Vel'Enar]]'s balance in nature as a call to sustain only what is needed.  
**Rituals**: During harvest, [[Lady Night]] organizes festivals of gratitude where offerings are made to honour [[Vel'Enar]]. These celebrations remind the faithful to honour the end of each cycle and appreciate what the earth has given.  
**Followers**: Farmers and communities come to [[Lady Night]] for blessings before harvests, seeking guidance on when and how much to take to ensure balance in nature's gifts.

# The Pantheon Hierarchy  

## [[Vel'Enar]] – The Twilight Guide (Head of the Pantheon)  
- Central figure overseeing the balance between life and death, transitions, and the eternal cycle.  
- All gods serve [[Vel'Enar]]'s greater purpose, representing specific facets of the cycle or supporting mortal life's various aspects.  

---

## The Primary Quadrants (Domains of Influence)  
The pantheon divides into four key areas of influence, each reflecting a vital aspect of existence. Each quadrant contains gods connected by theme or purpose.  

### 1. Nature and Renewal (Cycles of Growth and Change)  
- **[[Ceylira]] – Goddess of Nature and Growth**: Represents the natural world and its nurturing forces.  
- **[[Thalana]] – Goddess of Harvests**: Focuses on abundance, sustenance, and the culmination of growth.  
- **[[Veyradin]] – God of Fertility and Creation**: Oversees procreation, creativity, and the spark of life.  
- **[[Ilyra]] – Goddess of Light and Renewal**: Symbolizes healing, recovery, and hope after hardship.  

### 2. Mortality and the Mind (Wisdom, Emotion, and the Journey of Life)  
- **Nuloran – God of Wisdom and Knowledge**: Guides mortals with intellect and understanding.  
- **[[Rilana]] – Goddess of Love and Compassion**: Embodies emotional bonds and human connection.  
- **[[Lyneth]] – Goddess of Beauty and Joy**: Celebrates the pleasures and artistry of life.  
- **[[Caelith]] – God of Dreams and Prophecy**: Reveals the mysteries of the future and inner truths through visions.  

### 3. Strength and Survival (Resilience and Endurance)  
- **[[Torvin]] – God of Strength and Resilience**: Represents physical and emotional endurance.  
- **[[Thalana]] – Goddess of Harvests**: Shares a role here due to the importance of survival through sustenance.  
- **[[Veyradin]] – God of Fertility and Creation**: His influence spans survival through generative forces.  

### 4. Change and the Unknown (Secrets, Trickery, and Transformation)  
- **[[Ilhara]] – Goddess of Change and Trickery**: Represents the inevitability of change and the power of adaptation.  
- **[[Valtoris]] – God of Shadows and Secrets**: Embodies the hidden and mysterious aspects of life.  
- **[[Arvendris]] – God of War and Ambition**: Channels the raw energy of conflict and ambition as forces of transformation.  
- **[[Othelar]] – God of Death and Judgment**: Balances Ilhara and Valtoris by representing the final, impartial judgment in life's journey.  

---
## How They Connect to [[Vel'Enar]]  
Each god supports [[Vel'Enar]]'s philosophy of balance and transition:  
- **Nature and Renewal Quadrant**: Ensures the material world thrives within its natural cycles.  
- **Mortality and the Mind Quadrant**: Facilitates emotional, intellectual, and spiritual growth, essential to navigating life and death.  
- **Strength and Survival Quadrant**: Embodies resilience and the perseverance needed to endure life's trials.  
- **Change and the Unknown Quadrant**: Represents the transformative forces that push existence forward.  

---
## Interconnected Roles  
- **Rituals and Worship**: Each god's worshippers contribute to the cycle of life, death, and rebirth, reinforcing [[Vel'Enar]]'s overarching vision.  
- **Overlap of Domains**: The gods' domains often overlap, symbolizing that no aspect of existence is isolated. For example, [[Ceylira]] (growth) and [[Thalana]] (harvest) are connected as parts of the same natural cycle. Similarly, [[Rilana]] (love) and [[Arvendris]] (ambition) explore different sides of human desire.  
- **Balance of Forces**: Gods like [[Ilyra]] (light) and [[Valtoris]] (shadows) embody opposing yet complementary principles, mirroring [[Vel'Enar]]'s teachings about the unity of opposites.
