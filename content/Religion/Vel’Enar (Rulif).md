## Clergy and Worship  
Clerics and followers of Vel'Enar see their role as guides and mediators between life and death. They help people make peace with death, either through funerary rites or counseling the dying. They also celebrate births, viewing new life as the return of a soul to the material world after its time in another form. Rituals often take place at twilight, the time between day and night, symbolizing the transition between life and death. The changing seasons are also important to their worship, and many ceremonies are held at the equinoxes, when the balance between light and dark shifts.  
### Funerary Rites  
A typical rite involves placing the deceased on a bed of flowers and greenery, symbolizing the return to the earth, and lighting a candle to guide their soul into the next life.  
### Celebration of Life  
Followers often hold celebrations when new life enters the world, as they believe this is a sign of a soul returning from its journey in another realm.  
## [[High Lady Dawn]] (Funeral Rites)  
**Role**: As the overseer of funeral rites, High Lady Dawn guides souls from the world of the living to the world beyond. She embodies Vel'Enar’s principle of "Guide, Don’t Judge," leading the departed with peace and respect for their journey.  
**Rituals**: Dawn holds twilight vigils, where lanterns are lit to symbolize the path to the next life. These rites are gentle and serene, often accompanied by songs that recount the individual’s life journey.  
**Followers**: Those who find comfort in endings or have lost loved ones often seek her counsel, finding solace in her impartial perspective on death as a transition rather than a loss.  
## [[High Lady Dusk]] (Birth Rites)  
**Role**: High Lady Dusk is responsible for rites of birth, welcoming souls back into the cycle of life. She reflects the teaching "Honor the Journey," viewing each new life as a soul returning to the material world.  
**Rituals**: At dawn, infants are blessed with a drop of water mixed with soil to symbolize both growth and grounding. She tells families that each soul has returned for new experiences and learning.  
**Followers**: Families and expectant parents often turn to Dusk for blessings, as she embodies the cycle's renewal and provides wisdom on the roles of growth and rebirth.  
## [[Lady Mid]] (Lambing)  
**Role**: Lady Mid oversees lambing and livestock birth, aligning with Vel'Enar’s teachings to "Embrace the Cycle." She ensures animals are brought into the world with reverence, viewing them as part of the natural balance.  
**Rituals**: At each lambing, Mid recites verses of life’s cyclic nature and ensures offerings to Vel'Enar, thanking him for the vitality and nourishment these creatures bring. She believes they, too, contribute to the ongoing dance between life and death.  
**Followers**: Farmers and herders closely follow Lady Mid's guidance, relying on her knowledge for successful lambing and fostering a respectful relationship with the land.  
## [[Lady Night]] (Harvest)  
**Role**: Lady Night manages the harvest rites, upholding "Respect the Balance" by gathering what nature provides without excess. She interprets Vel'Enar’s balance in nature as a call to sustain only what is needed.  
**Rituals**: During harvest, Lady Night organizes festivals of gratitude where offerings are made to honor Vel'Enar. These celebrations remind the faithful to honor the end of each cycle and appreciate what the earth has given.  
**Followers**: Farmers and communities come to Lady Night for blessings before harvests, seeking guidance on when and how much to take to ensure balance in nature’s gifts.
