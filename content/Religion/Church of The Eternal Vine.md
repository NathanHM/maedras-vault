## Overview  
The Eternal Vine is a deep-rooted, nature-based religion that focuses on the veneration of life, harvest, and fertility. It is central to [[Alithi|Alithi]]'s cultural identity, particularly in the rural, agrarian regions. The Eternal Vine reveres the interconnectedness of all living things, symbolized by a vine that grows through every corner of existence, linking life, death, and rebirth. The religion emphasizes respect for the land and community, and its followers view the harvest season as the time to honour the earth's fertility.  
## Key Beliefs  
- **Cycle of Life and Death**: Every life is part of a greater cycle. Just as the vine flourishes in spring and falls in autumn, individuals too are born, live, and die, contributing to a greater cosmic order.  
- **Community and Fertility**: Priests are highly involved in agricultural rites, such as the spring planting and fall harvests, which are considered sacred acts.  
- **Ancestor Worship**: Ancestors are honoured through rituals, particularly at harvest time. Their wisdom is believed to flow down through the vine, influencing future generations.  
## Practices  
- **Sacred Rituals**: Communal prayers during the planting and harvesting seasons ask for blessings and protection from the spirits of the land.  
- **Harvest Festivals**: Massive celebrations of prosperity and the gifts of the earth, including rites of passage for youth to learn the values of the Vine.  
- **Sacrificial Offerings**: Mostly symbolic offerings of crops or livestock. Some zealous sects may practice minor blood sacrifices during times of crisis.  
## Influence in [[Alithi|Alithi]]
- **Cultural Pillar**: The Eternal Vine religion shapes [[Alithi|Alithi]]'s laws, particularly those regarding agricultural work and communal life. It has a strong presence among [[Alithi|Alithi]]'s traditionalist factions, often standing in opposition to Regent [[Halric Dain]]'s more secular, militaristic reforms.
- **Political Tension**: While the Eternal Vine is mostly associated with rural, agrarian communities, its power in the capital is beginning to wane, as [[Halric Dain]] and his allies push for a more modernized, industrialized [[Alithi|Alithi]].
