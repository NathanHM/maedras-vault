## Core Tenets of Worship  
1. **Embrace the Wild**: Followers believe that to truly live, one must reconnect with their primal instincts. The wilderness is sacred, and survival in its harshness is seen as both a test and a gift. Strength, cunning, and the thrill of the hunt are central to [[Virelya]]'s teachings.  
2. **Indulge and Release**: Virelya's followers are encouraged to partake in ecstatic festivals, where they revel in music, food, drink, and physical pleasure. These celebrations serve as a release valve, freeing them from the constraints of their daily, rigid lives. Festivals are seen as a necessary counterbalance to the harsh discipline they otherwise adhere to.  
3. **The Dance Between Life and Death**: Virelya is a god of life and death intertwined. The hunt is sacred because it represents both the preservation of life and the inevitability of death. Sacrifice and blood rituals are common, but they are performed with an understanding that death feeds new life.  
4. **Frenzy and Transformation**: Ecstatic rituals involving intoxicants (mushrooms, wine, or herbal concoctions) are believed to bring worshippers closer to Virelya. In these altered states, participants might experience frenzied visions or transformations, symbolic of their closeness to the god.  

## Clergy and Followers  
- **Worshippers**: Farmers, hunters, warriors, and revellers in [[Qavrud]] all pay tribute to Virelya. The god's duality speaks to the practical needs of survival (agriculture, hunting, and healing through nature) and the spiritual need for release (festivals and ritualistic indulgence).  
- **Druids and Shamans**: Virelya's primary clergy are druids and shamans who serve as both spiritual leaders and survival experts. They oversee the sacred hunts, conduct agricultural blessings, and lead festivals where the community can indulge in frenzied, primal worship.  
- **Festivals and Blood Rituals**: The Festival of the Wild Hunt is an annual event where followers dress in animal pelts, hunt wild beasts, and offer their blood or sacrifice to Virelya. This hunt is followed by an extravagant feast and wild revelry, during which intoxication and indulgence in food, drink, and dance are encouraged.  

## Festivals and Rites  
1. **The Great Wild Hunt**: A large-scale hunt in which followers of Virelya, led by the shamans, track and kill a wild beast as a ritual offering. The prey, often the most dangerous animal in the area, symbolizes the raw, untamed spirit of nature, and its death is seen as a sacrifice to renew the land.  
2. **The Night of Ecstasy**: A festival held under a full moon where the people of Qavrud consume hallucinogenic plants and alcohol, dancing and feasting deep into the night. It is believed that during these moments, Virelya blesses the revellers with visions and frenzied strength, which can unlock primal instincts or arcane power.  
3. **Blood of the Earth**: A more sombre, grounded rite where followers offer their own blood to the soil during harvest season. This ritual ensures the land remains fertile, blessed by both the life and death forces that Virelya represents.  

## Religious Influence in Qavrud  
In theocratic Qavrud, Virelya plays a crucial role in maintaining balance. While much of the society is conservative, rigid, and hierarchical, Virelya's chaotic influence is channelled into structured rituals and festivals. This allows the people to indulge in chaos safely, reinforcing the balance between control and freedom, civilization and wilderness.  
- **Religious Leaders**: The Priests of Virelya, known as Frenzykeepers, have a unique role in Qavrud society. They serve as both spiritual guides and wild, unpredictable figures who embody Virelya's dual aspects of order and chaos. Their advice is sought in matters of survival, agriculture, and conflict, but they are also the overseers of wild celebrations.  
- **Cultural Practices**: Qavrud's worship of Virelya creates a complex relationship with the natural world. While they cultivate and care for the land, they also believe in leaving parts of it untouched, wild, and free to grow as nature dictates. Similarly, they engage in frenzied blood sacrifices during specific festivals to renew the land and honour its wildness.  
- **Social Influence**: Virelya's festivals are a release valve for Qavrud's strict society. The theocratic rulers allow these indulgent practices because they prevent unrest, letting the people give in to primal chaos for a time before returning to their disciplined lives. However, there is also tension, as the more devout and conservative factions of the theocracy fear the frenzied nature of Virelya's worship.
