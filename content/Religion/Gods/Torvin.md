# The Stoneborn, God of Strength and Resilience
### Alignment
Lawful Neutral
### Domains
- Strength
- Protection
- Earth
### Symbol
A mountain split by a river
### Portfolio
- Physical resilience
- Protection
- Endurance
- Stability
### Worshippers
- Warriors
- Builders
- Those enduring hardship
- Defenders of family and land
## Philosophy
Torvin teaches that strength lies in resilience and that enduring hardship leads to growth. He believes in protecting those in need, with fortitude being life's greatest virtue.
## Personality
Strong, loyal, and protective, Torvin is calm and steady, valuing persistence and loyalty.
## Appearance
Torvin is a towering figure, often armored, with an unyielding gaze. He embodies the strength of mountains.
## Tenets
- **Protect and Endure**: Stand firm for what you value.
- **Embrace Resilience**: Hardship is a teacher; overcome with grace.
- **Stand United**: True strength comes from community and unity.
## Clergy and Worship
Torvin's clergy train warriors and defend the helpless. Worship involves acts of resilience, such as tests of endurance.
## Rituals
- **Rite of Strength**: A test of endurance honoring Torvin, undertaken by his followers.
- **Shielding Ceremony**: A blessing for those about to enter battle or undertake trials.
