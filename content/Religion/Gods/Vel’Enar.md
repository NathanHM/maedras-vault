# The Twilight Guide, Shepherd of the Eternal Path

### Alignment
Neutral
### Domains
- Life
- Grave
- Knowledge
### Symbol
A setting sun over a horizon, with a small sprouting plant below it, representing both the end and the beginning.
### Portfolio
- Cycles of life and death
- Transitions
- Rebirth
- Balance
- Twilight (the in-between of life and death)
### Worshippers
- Those who seek peace in the inevitability of death
- Druids who value natural cycles
- Clerics who help guide souls
- Those who see life and death as two halves of the same coin
## Philosophy
Vel'Enar teaches that death is not to be feared but understood as a necessary part of the soul's journey. Life and death are two aspects of a single, greater cycle. Vel'Enar’s followers believe that just as night follows day, death follows life, only for the sun to rise again, just as the soul continues its journey in another form or plane.
Vel'Enar represents the idea that death is a transition, not a final verdict, and that after death comes a new beginning, be it a new life, a transformation, or a passage to another state of existence. Vel'Enar doesn’t distinguish between good or evil in death—it comes to all in due time, but each transition is treated as a step toward growth.
## Personality
Vel'Enar is calm, patient, and endlessly compassionate, yet they are also unsentimental about death. They view the fears and attachments of mortals to their current existence as fleeting, knowing that a greater journey lies beyond. Vel'Enar speaks softly of the beauty in endings and beginnings alike, encouraging followers to embrace the unknown.
They are often depicted as walking along twilight paths between worlds, offering a hand to the souls who have completed one stage of their existence and are ready to move to the next. Their presence brings comfort, not because they deny the sorrow of loss, but because they promise that the journey continues.
## Appearance
Vel'Enar is often portrayed as an androgynous figure draped in twilight-colored robes (shades of deep purple, gray, and amber), their form shifting between youth and age, reflecting the eternal cycle of life and death. They are sometimes shown holding a lantern or a staff adorned with growing vines and dying leaves.
## Tenets
1. **Embrace the Cycle**: Life and death are two sides of the same coin. Cherish life, but fear not the end—it is merely the start of a new journey.
2. **Guide, Don’t Judge**: Death is not to be met with punishment or reward but as a transition. Help others find peace in the end and guide them to the next phase.
3. **Respect the Balance**: Life and death must remain in balance. To unnaturally prolong life or to cause needless death is to disrupt the cycle.
4. **Honor the Journey**: Every soul has a path. Walk your own with grace and help others along theirs, whether they are in the prime of life or near the end.
