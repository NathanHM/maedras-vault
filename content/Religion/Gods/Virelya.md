## Appearance  
A satyr-shaped man with wild eyes, dark brown legs, curled horns, and deep forest green skin  
## Concept and Worship  
Virelya represents the dual nature of life: on one hand, the wild, primal instincts that drive creatures to hunt, fight, and survive in the untamed wilderness; on the other, the liberating and intoxicating joy of celebration, indulgence, and abandon. Qavrud's rigid, hierarchical society embraces Virelya because the god offers a controlled outlet for both their fierce survivalism and their need to release tension through exuberant celebration.
