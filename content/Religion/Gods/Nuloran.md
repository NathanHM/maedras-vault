# The Tempest Rider, God of Storms and Sea  
### Alignment  
Chaotic Neutral  
### Domains  
- Tempest  
- Nature  
### Symbol  
A crashing wave with a bolt of lightning above it  
### Portfolio  
- Oceans  
- Storms  
- Natural forces  
- Freedom  
- Unpredictability  
### Worshippers  
- Sailors  
- Fishermen  
- Coastal communities  
- Those who embrace freedom and adventure  
## Philosophy  
Nuloran embodies the fierce, untamable spirit of nature. He teaches that true freedom comes from embracing the wild and unpredictable, for life’s greatest challenges often lead to profound strength and understanding.  
## Personality  
Dynamic and fierce, Nuloran is both liberating and fearsome. He is a spirited force who values freedom and respects only those who can withstand his power.  
## Appearance  
Nuloran appears as a tall, weathered figure wrapped in storm clouds, with piercing eyes like lightning over the sea.  
## Tenets  
- **Embrace the Unknown**: The sea is wild, and so is life; face challenges with courage.  
- **Freedom Above All**: Do not seek to tame the world but to navigate it with respect.  
- **Respect Nature’s Power**: Nature is both creator and destroyer; honor its cycles.  
## Clergy and Worship  
Nuloran’s clergy are wild and free-spirited, often acting as guides in coastal areas. Worship includes sea offerings and rituals that ask for safe passage.  
## Rituals  
- **The Tide’s Offering**: Sailors toss a token or gift into the sea, asking for protection and favorable tides.  
- **Storm’s Challenge**: A rite of passage for followers, often involving a task performed during harsh weather, symbolizing their resilience.
