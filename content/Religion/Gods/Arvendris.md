# The Songkeeper, Goddess of Art and Inspiration
### Alignment  
Chaotic Good  
### Domains  
- Light  
- Knowledge  
### Symbol  
A lyre with radiating lines  
### Portfolio  
- Music  
- Art  
- Creativity  
- Inspiration  
### Worshippers  
- Artists  
- Musicians  
- Storytellers  
- Those who inspire others  
## Philosophy  
Arvendris teaches that art is a bridge to the soul, capable of uplifting and connecting people. She values creativity as a divine expression of the spirit.  
## Personality  
Passionate and expressive, Arvendris is often exuberant, encouraging all to explore their creative talents.  
## Appearance  
Arvendris is depicted as a lively figure with a lyre, adorned in flowing robes of vibrant colors, symbolizing endless creativity.  
## Tenets  
- **Create Boldly**: Art is a reflection of the soul; do not fear expression.  
- **Inspire Others**: Use your gifts to uplift and connect.  
- **Celebrate Beauty**: Life is art; appreciate and honor its beauty.  
## Clergy and Worship  
Arvendris’ clergy are often performers or patrons of the arts, leading celebrations that inspire creativity.  
## Rituals  
- **Song of the Heart**: A performance-based ritual where followers create or perform art in Arvendris’ honor.  
- **The Muse’s Gift**: An offering to Arvendris, seeking inspiration for a creative endeavor.
