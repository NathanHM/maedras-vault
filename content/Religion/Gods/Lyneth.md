# The Serene Healer, Goddess of Compassion and Peace
### Alignment  
Neutral Good  
### Domains  
- Life  
- Peace  
### Symbol  
A lily encircled by calm waters  
### Portfolio  
- Healing  
- Compassion  
- Tranquility  
- Conflict resolution  
### Worshippers  
- Healers  
- Mediators  
- Caretakers  
- Those striving for peace  
## Philosophy  
Lyneth teaches that peace and compassion are the ultimate goals, healing both body and spirit. She encourages empathy and understanding, seeking to end suffering and promote harmony.  
## Personality  
Calm, gentle, and nurturing, Lyneth is a comforting presence who values peace above all.  
## Appearance  
Lyneth is often depicted in flowing robes, her expression serene and gentle, with a touch that radiates calm.  
## Tenets  
- **Heal with Kindness**: Bring comfort to those in need.  
- **Promote Peace**: Seek to end conflict and encourage understanding.  
- **Compassion Above All**: Approach others with empathy and gentleness.  
## Clergy and Worship  
Lyneth's clergy are often medics and peacekeepers, mediating conflicts and promoting wellness. Worship includes healing rituals and peacemaking ceremonies.  
## Rituals  
- **Balm of Serenity**: A blessing given to those in emotional turmoil or physical pain.  
- **The Tranquil Accord**: A ceremonial offering for peace between feuding parties.
