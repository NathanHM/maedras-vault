Lawful Good

- Light  
### Symbol  
A blazing silver flame over scales  
### Portfolio  
- Justice

### Worshippers  
- Judges  
- Lawkeepers  
- Protectors  
- Those seeking justice  
## Philosophy  
Veyradin teaches that justice is the foundation of a peaceful society and that honor and truth must be upheld. He stands as a beacon of fairness, protecting the innocent and punishing the guilty.  
## Personality  
Stern and fair, Veyradin is a figure of unwavering morality, valuing justice above all else.  
## Appearance  
Veyradin is a tall, armored figure, his face serious and unwavering, with a torch in one hand and scales in the other.  
## Tenets  
- **Honor Above All**: Stay true to your word and uphold fairness.  
- **Protect the Innocent**: Justice must shield those in need.  
- **Seek Truth Relentlessly**: Lies and deceit undermine justice.  
## Clergy and Worship  
Veyradin's clergy are advocates for law and justice, often mediating disputes. Worship includes oath-swearing ceremonies and seeking Veyradin's blessing in legal matters.
