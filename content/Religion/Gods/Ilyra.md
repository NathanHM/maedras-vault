### Worshippers
- Midwives
- Farmers
- Travelers
- Those embarking on new ventures
- Anyone seeking a fresh start
## Philosophy
Ilyra teaches that every ending is an opportunity for a new beginning. She believes in fostering hope, growth, and resilience among her followers, encouraging them to see light even in dark times. Followers of Ilyra view life as a gift and each day as a chance to start anew.
## Personality
Ilyra is warm, nurturing, and optimistic, often portrayed as endlessly compassionate and ever-hopeful. She encourages her followers to embrace their potential and find strength in life’s rebirths. Her presence brings comfort, reminding mortals that there is always light ahead.
## Appearance
Ilyra appears as a radiant figure surrounded by soft golden light, often depicted with flowers in her hair. Her form shifts to reflect a sunrise, symbolizing her dominion over beginnings.
## Tenets
- **Embrace Growth**: Just as plants grow toward the light, so should you reach for your potential.
- **Radiate Hope**: Seek to inspire those around you to overcome darkness and look toward the future.
- **Cherish Life**: Each life is precious, and each day holds the promise of renewal.
## Clergy and Worship
Clerics of Ilyra often lead dawn rituals, celebrating new life and fostering community growth. They oversee birthing rites and ceremonies marking life milestones. Seasonal rites mark the planting and harvest cycles, reflecting her connection to nature's renewal.
## Rituals
- **Blessing of Newborns**: A ceremony to welcome and bless new life, symbolizing a fresh beginning under Ilyra’s light.
