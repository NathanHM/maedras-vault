# The Veiled Dreamer, Goddess of Night and Secrets
### Alignment  
Chaotic Neutral  
### Domains  
- Knowledge  
- Trickery  
### Symbol  
A crescent moon partially obscured by clouds  
### Portfolio  
- Dreams  
- Secrets  
- Mysteries  
- Hidden knowledge  
### Worshippers  
- Mystics  
- Dreamers  
- Spies  
- Those seeking hidden truths  
## Philosophy  
Thalana teaches that the world holds countless secrets beneath its surface. She encourages curiosity and exploration of the unknown, believing that dreams and mysteries hold untold power.  
## Personality  
Elusive and curious, Thalana enjoys the dance of secrets and shadows, often revealing only hints.  
## Appearance  
Thalana is portrayed as a darkly cloaked figure with glowing eyes, symbolizing both clarity and enigma.  
## Tenets  
- **Seek the Unseen**: Knowledge lies in the shadows; be willing to search for it.  
- **Truths Are Veiled**: Not all is as it seems; question appearances.  
- **Embrace Mystery**: Secrets are treasures; uncover them with reverence.  
## Clergy and Worship  
Thalana's clergy often work as advisors, dream interpreters, or spies. Worship includes rituals to seek guidance through dreams or uncover hidden truths.  
## Rituals  
- **Dreamer's Night**: A ceremony inviting Thalana to share visions or omens through dreams.  
- **The Shadow's Insight**: A ritual for those seeking hidden knowledge, involving meditation or symbolic offerings.
