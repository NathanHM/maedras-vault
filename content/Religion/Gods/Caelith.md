# The Keeper of Mysteries, Goddess of Knowledge and Memory
### Alignment
True Neutral
### Domains
- Knowledge
- Arcana
- Trickery
### Symbol
An open book with an eye at its center
### Portfolio
- Secrets
- Wisdom
- Memory
- Hidden truths
- Riddles
### Worshippers
- Scholars
- Historians
- Spies
- Those seeking wisdom or hidden knowledge
## Philosophy
Caelith believes in the preservation of knowledge and the mystery of life’s secrets. Followers are encouraged to seek truth but respect what is hidden, understanding that wisdom can come from both light and shadow.
## Personality
Caelith is both curious and secretive, delighted by riddles and mysteries. She values those who seek knowledge but is cautious about revealing it too freely.
## Appearance
Often depicted with a book and an owl, Caelith appears as an aged figure cloaked in dark robes, her gaze penetrating.
## Tenets
- **Seek Knowledge, Respect Secrets**: Pursue wisdom, but be cautious with what you learn.
- **Honor Memory**: Preserve what is important, for memory binds all things.
- **Delight in Mysteries**: Life’s mysteries are as valuable as the truths they contain.
## Clergy and Worship
Caelith’s clergy are both keepers and seekers of secrets. Their temples are places of study, and their worship involves meditative silence.
## Rituals
- **Veil of Silence**: A rite where followers meditate in silence, seeking to uncover hidden truths.
- **Remembrance**: A yearly gathering where stories and wisdom are shared and preserved.
