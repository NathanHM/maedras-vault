# The Weaver of Fates, Goddess of Destiny and Fortune
### Alignment  
True Neutral  
### Domains  
- Knowledge  
- Trickery  
### Symbol  
A web with strands that crisscross infinitely  
### Portfolio  
- Fate  
- Fortune  
- Luck  
- Choices  
### Worshippers  
- Scholars  
- Those seeking guidance on life paths  
- Gamblers  
- Travelers  
## Philosophy  
Ilhara teaches that all things are connected through the threads of fate. She values choice, acknowledging that while fate may guide, it is up to each individual to navigate their path.  
## Personality  
Mysterious and introspective, Ilhara watches the weave of fate with calm acceptance, believing each choice forms a new thread.  
## Appearance  
Ilhara is depicted as a veiled figure with a silver-threaded web in her hands, each thread glistening with possibility.  
## Tenets  
- **Respect Destiny, Embrace Choice**: Destiny shapes the path, but choice gives it meaning.  
- **Fortune is Fickle**: Luck can change with a breath; accept it with grace.  
- **Weave Your Path**: Take responsibility for the choices you make.  
## Clergy and Worship  
Ilhara’s clergy are advisors and oracles, helping people understand their destinies. Worship involves casting lots or reading omens.  
## Rituals  
- **Thread of Fate**: A ritual in which followers tie a thread to represent a desired path or choice.  
- **The Wheel of Fortune**: A ceremonial spinning of a wheel to seek Ilhara’s guidance on a matter of luck or destiny.
