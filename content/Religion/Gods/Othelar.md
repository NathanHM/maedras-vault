# The Flameheart, God of Passion and Valor
### Alignment  
Chaotic Good  
### Domains  
- War  
- Light  
### Symbol  
A flaming sword crossed with a lion’s paw  
### Portfolio  
- Courage  
- Passion  
- Strength in battle  
- Righteous fury  
### Worshippers  
- Warriors  
- Knights  
- Those fighting for justice  
- Defenders of the weak  
## Philosophy  
Othelar teaches that true courage is ignited by passion and purpose. He values bold action and bravery in the face of adversity, especially when defending others.  
## Personality  
Fierce and protective, Othelar is a bold figure who inspires those who fight for justice and others’ safety.  
## Appearance  
Othelar is depicted as a muscular, fiery-eyed figure wielding a flaming sword, symbolizing valor and strength.  
## Tenets  
- **Fight with Honor**: Protect the weak and uphold justice.  
- **Embrace Your Passion**: Channel your inner fire into courage and righteous action.  
- **Stand Fearless**: Face challenges with unwavering resolve.  
## Clergy and Worship  
Othelar’s clergy are warriors and defenders, offering blessings to soldiers and leading battle prayers.  
## Rituals  
- **Valor’s Flame**: A ritual before battle where warriors are blessed for courage.  
- **Fury of Justice**: A ceremony invoking Othelar’s protection for those seeking to fight for justice.
