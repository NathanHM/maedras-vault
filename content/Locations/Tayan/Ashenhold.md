## The Fortress of Ashenhold - Lore and History
Perched high in the jagged peaks of [[Tayan]]’s northern mountains, the Fortress of Ashenhold is a grim and imposing bastion. Known for its cold stone walls, fierce winds, and perpetual mountain mists, Ashenhold is a vital military stronghold — but beneath its foundations lies something far older and far more dangerous.
The fortress was unknowingly constructed over the ruins of an ancient site tied to the lost [[Vath'ira]] civilization — a people believed to have harnessed fire magic and powerful elemental forces before their catastrophic fall.
## Origins of the Ruins
The ancient structure beneath Ashenhold is believed to have been a temple or vault built by the Vath'ira during their height. Known only in fragmented records as the Pyre Vault, this site was said to house a powerful artifact — a shard of living flame that Vath'iran priests believed was a gift from the heavens. (See [[Collected Myths and Legends of the Known Realms#1. The Pyre's Breath|The Pyre's Breath]] and [[Floral Myths and Legends of the Known Realms#3. The Bloom of First Light|The Bloom of the First Light]])
This shard, sometimes called the Heart of Cinders, was said to burn with unnatural heat, capable of igniting stone and searing magic itself. The Vath'irans worshipped the flame as a divine symbol of both power and rebirth. Some scholars suspect the Heart of Cinders was a fragment of [[the Emberheart]] itself, tied to the very entity [[the Eternal Ember Cult]] now venerates. (See [[War of the Ember#The Night of Cinders|The Night of Cinders]])
When Vath'ira fell, the Pyre Vault was abandoned, and the flames within are said to have died — or perhaps been sealed away.
## The Rise of Ashenhold
Centuries later, during a period of border skirmishes with [[Rulif]], Tayan’s leaders ordered the construction of a fortress to control a key mountain pass. The site chosen — unknowingly the location of the Pyre Vault — seemed to radiate warmth even in the dead of winter.
During construction, workers reported discovering carved stone tablets and ash-filled chambers deep beneath the earth. Fearing they had disturbed some forgotten curse, they sealed the ruins away and built the fortress above it. Only fragments of these discoveries survived — scattered symbols resembling the runes the players [[Session 14|encountered]] in the [[Scorched Vath'iran Ruins|Scorched Ruins]] of Vath'ira.
## Legends and Rumours
Many believe the fortress’s unsettling warmth is a lingering curse from the Vath'iran ruins below. Over the centuries, rumours have persisted:
1. **The Embered Knights**: Legends claim that Ashenhold’s most elite soldiers are unknowingly tied to the ruins' forgotten power. Some say the fortress’s warriors feel a growing pull toward violence and fire, as if something in the mountain stirs their blood.
2. **The Everburning Forge**: Deep within the fortress is a forge said to burn without fuel. The forge’s flames glow a dull red, and weapons crafted there are rumoured to strike with unnatural heat. ([[Vladimir Vladishkov|Vlad's sword?]]) Some whisper that the forge is secretly linked to the slumbering Heart of Cinders beneath the fortress.
3. **The Whispering Ashes**: Soldiers stationed at Ashenhold speak of strange whispers drifting through the fortress halls at night. These whispers grow louder near the old sealed stairway that leads down into the forgotten ruins. ([[Floral Myths and Legends of the Known Realms|Gravegrasp?]])
4. **The Scorched Stonemason**: A chilling tale tells of a stonemason who vanished during the fortress’s construction. Days later, his body was found burned to cinders — yet the stone floor beneath him was unmarked.
