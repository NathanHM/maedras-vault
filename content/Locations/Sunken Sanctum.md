The Sunken Sanctum is a place of forgotten power and whispered dread. Once a temple, later a prison, and now a ruin shrouded in mystery, it is deeply tied to the fate of [[the Emberheart]] and the rise of the [[Cult of the Eternal Ember|Eternal Ember Cult]]. Located near the coastal town of [[Stormhaven]] in [[Rulif]], the sanctum’s presence looms large in local folklore.
## Origins of the Sanctum
The Sunken Sanctum was originally a place of worship dedicated to the divine balance between fire and renewal. Early [[Worship of Vel'Enar (Rulif)|followers of Vel'Enar]] believed fire was both a tool of destruction and rebirth — a force to be respected, but never worshipped.
The temple’s original keepers, known as the [[Wardens of the Ember]], maintained this balance. They revered a powerful elemental force known as [[the Emberheart]], which they believed embodied both divine warmth and chaotic fire. The Wardens carefully contained [[the Emberheart]] within the sanctum’s depths, ensuring its power was never unleashed.
## The Drowning Curse
Legends claim the water that now fills the lower sanctum is no longer ordinary seawater. Known as the Drowned Veil, this dark, brackish liquid is said to shift unnaturally, sometimes rising and falling without the pull of the tides. Local sailors speak of strange lights flickering beneath the waves and whispers echoing from the shoreline. 
Some believe the spirits of the fallen Wardens still haunt the flooded chambers, guarding [[the Emberheart]]'s prison. Others claim [[the Emberheart]]’s influence has corrupted the waters, turning them into a conduit for its growing power.
