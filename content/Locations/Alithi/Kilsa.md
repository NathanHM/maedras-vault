Kilsa is a border town, located on the edge of [[Rulif]] and [[Alithi]]

---
## Transport Hub
- The Kilsa transport hub is managed by [[Bilbo Frankenfert]]
- The hub has a secret, older [[Transport Circles|circle]] in the basement that is connected to the [[Scorched Vath'iran Ruins]]
## Monastery of [[The Eternal Vine]]
- The monastery is where the party went to meet [[Father Ellric]]
- They also met here with [[General David McIver]]
## Green Soldier
- The Green Soldier is a tavern where a secret recruitment event was held by [[The Eternal Ember Cult]]
## Pink Lotus
- The Pink Lotus is a brothel in Kilsa
- Here the party met [[Rose]]
- They [[Session 13|discussed]] a job protecting [[The Caravan Raids|caravans]] for [[Lord Tom Macavoy]]
## Purple Tulip
- The Purple Tulip is the tavern where the party first stayed upon arriving in Kilsa
- They were offered a week's stay by Bilbo
## Yellow Duck
- The Yellow Duck is a tavern where [[High Lady Dawn]] stayed after she escaped the cult.
- [[Gilbert]] unintentionally destroyed one of the rooms by summoning an elephant
## Queries Queries
- Queries Queries is a scholar's tower, containing a public library
