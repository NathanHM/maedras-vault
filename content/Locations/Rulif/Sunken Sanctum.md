## Lore of the Sunken Sanctum
The Sunken Sanctum is a place of forgotten power and whispered dread. Once a temple, later a prison, and now a ruin shrouded in mystery, it is deeply tied to the fate of [[the Emberheart]] and the rise of the [[The Eternal Ember Cult|Eternal Ember Cult]]. Located near the coastal town of [[Stormhaven]] in [[Rulif]], the sanctum’s presence looms large in local folklore.
### Origins of the Sanctum
The Sunken Sanctum was originally a place of worship dedicated to the divine balance between fire and renewal. Early [[Motherhood of Vel'Enar|followers of Vel'Enar]] believed fire was both a tool of destruction and rebirth — a force to be respected, but never worshipped.
The temple’s original keepers, known as the [[The Eternal Ember Cult#Wardens of the Ember|Wardens of the Ember]], maintained this balance. They revered a powerful elemental force known as the Emberheart, which they believed embodied both divine warmth and chaotic fire. The Wardens carefully contained the Emberheart within the sanctum’s depths, ensuring its power was never unleashed.
### The War of the Ember
Centuries ago, a splinter sect — the forerunners of [[the Eternal Ember Cult]] — believed [[the Emberheart]] was not a force to be [[Sunken Sanctum|contained]], but a slumbering god that could purify the world through flame.
This sparked the [[War of the Ember]], a brutal conflict that nearly reduced the region to ash. The cult sought to release the Emberheart, believing it would purge the "unworthy" and reshape the world in fire.
In a desperate bid to contain the threat, the [[The Eternal Ember Cult#Wardens of the Ember|Wardens]] channelled powerful magic, transforming the temple into a prison — a labyrinth of runes and wards that locked the Emberheart within a submerged chamber. The temple’s lower halls were flooded with seawater, believed to suppress the god’s fiery influence. The remaining Wardens sealed themselves inside, sacrificing their lives to reinforce the binding magic.
### The Drowning Curse
Legends claim the water that now fills the lower sanctum is no longer ordinary seawater. Known as the Drowned Veil, this dark, brackish liquid is said to shift unnaturally, sometimes rising and falling without the pull of the tides. Local sailors speak of strange lights flickering beneath the waves and whispers echoing from the shoreline. 
Some believe the spirits of the fallen Wardens still haunt the flooded chambers, guarding the Emberheart's prison. Others claim the Emberheart’s influence has corrupted the waters, turning them into a conduit for its growing power.
