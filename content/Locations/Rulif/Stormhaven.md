Stormhaven is a city located off the coast of [[Rulif]]. Stormhaven is where the party first formed, where they first encountered [[High Lady Dawn]] and uncovered the plot of [[The Missing Children]].

## The Mausoleum
- The mausoleum was where the cult held [[Evangaline]] when she was first [[The Missing Children|kidnapped]]
- The mausoleum had a [[Transport Circles|circle]] that one member of [[The Eternal Ember Cult]] used to escape
- The mausoleum was watched over by a cockroach, Roachy, first for the cult, then for the party
## House de Beauvoir
- The House de Beauvoir was the home of [[Lady Bly]]
- It was haunted by the ghost of her step-mother, Nonna
- The party convinced Nonna to leave the house if they brought Lady Bly to justice

## Wander o Wonder
- Wander o Wonder is an adventurers shop, offering weapons and services
