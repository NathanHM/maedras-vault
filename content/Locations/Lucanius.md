# Notable Buildings & Locations
## Central Districts
- ### **The Summit Hall** (Wealth District): 
	- Towering marble structure where the peace summit will occur. Guards posted at all times.  
- ### **Palace of Glass Rains** (Lencone):
	- Ancient palace with fountains that collect rain from enchanted domes. Residence of [[Alithi]]'s ruling family.  
- ### **Elaric Voss's Office** (Styerburn): 
	- Elegant bureaucratic tower filled with scrolls, treaties, and paranoid aides.  
## South Districts
- ### The White Swan Coffee House: 
	- Known for its high ceilings, imported beans, and poetry readings. Cult activity suspected in shipments.  
- ### Niconbury Tavern (“The Busted Halberd”): 
	- Where many off-duty guards and spies drink. Zellin Farro is often found here.  
- ### Old Graveyard of Saint Immurel (Honi): 
	- Overgrown cemetery with tunnels and crypts below. Local legends say [[Rumours from Rexius#Echoes in the Graveyard – Graveyard, Honi|spirits whisper at night]].  
- ### **Warehouse Row** (Honi):
	- Abandoned and active buildings side-by-side. [[Cult of the Eternal Ember|Cult]] has infiltrated some for use as secret meeting points.  
- ###  **The Salttooth Docks** (Honi): 
	Controlled by merchant factions. Graye Mender works here and can direct to [[The Missing Children|lost or smuggled children]].
## Western Districts
- ### **Ivyhall Flower Market** (Yonebury): 
	- Colourful and chaotic. [[Reeva]] has a modest stall filled with strange plants.  
- ### **Temple of the Four Lights** (Corat): 
	- A shrine to [[Vel'Enar]] with each wing representing Dawn, Dusk, Mid, and Night.  
- ### **Rintrer’s Step**: 
	- A watchtower overlooking ruins outside the city. Used for scouting — may see strange smoke in the hills.  
## Eastern Districts
- ### **The Forgotten Archive** (Espr Quarter): 
	- Partially condemned library filled with chained books. [[Maedra Lurith|Maedra]]’s favourite haunt.  
- ### **Sunmore Amphitheater**: 
	- Crumbling marble stage now used for rallies and protests. Recent [[Cult of the Eternal Ember|cult]] graffiti discovered. (Sunmore was largely destroyed in [[The Ashen Uprising]])  
- ### **Pharigg Redoubt**: 
	- Military outpost — the [[Cult of the Eternal Ember|cult]] is slowly infiltrating through lesser guardsmen.  
## Northern Districts
- ### **Statue of Alith the Bright** (Kashe): 
	- A revered national monument. [[Filos Tren]] can be found giving speeches nearby.  
- ### **Dwucliff Market Street**: 
	- Known for magical wares and knockoff relics. Always watched by both city guard and spies. Contains shops [[Bottles and Brooms]] and [[Wonders]].
