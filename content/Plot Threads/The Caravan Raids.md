# Involvement
## Tom Macavoy's Entertainers
- When in [[Kilsa]], the party were offered a job to escort a caravan of entertainers to [[Lucanius]] by [[Lord Tom Macavoy]]. The caravans had recently been under threat by raids. ([[Session 13]])
- This turned out to be a ploy, Macavoy was in on the raids and working with the perpetrators. The adventurers hired as escorts would then come under a clause in the magical contracts they signed and be forced to take the place of the entertainers.
- The magic enforcing these contracts was provided by the Emberheart, with possibly another force in play.
- Macavoy's scheme ended when he was killed by [[Rose]] and her party, the adventurers he had previously enslaved.
## Thorne and Tharrows Raids
- The party learned that Merinth Tharrows was the organisational force behind the caravan raids.
- Rellis Thorne was revealed to be providing the muscle for the raids via her mercenary company, the Black Blades.
- These raids were not being carried out in the vicinity of the [[Ashenhold]], suggesting the involvement of [[The Eternal Ember Cult]].
