[[Gilbert]] and [[Cyrena]], two half-elves, were both raised by single mothers. [[Juliana]] was never willing to discuss Gilbert's father, and Cyrena was never interested to ask about hers. When on the way to Lucanius, some magic from [[High Lady Dawn]] confirmed that the two were biological siblings, meaning they must share a father.

# Suspects
## Parties' Suspects
### [[The Emberheart]]
- In the [[Scorched Vath'iran Ruins]], the party find ancient depictions of an elven man, later suspected to be the Emberheart. Due to [[Wynona]]'s face blindness, she believes this figure to be Gilbert. ([[Session 15]])
- Later on, Gilbert pretends to be the Emberheart and successfully convinces a Flamekeeper that he is. ([[Session 17 18]])
- The Emberheart possessed Ferox, and may also possess Kaedric. If either is Gilbert's father, they may have been possessed by the Emberheart at the time making Gilbert it's child in some way.
### [[Lord High Priest Ferox Thal]]
- Ferox is first raised as a suspect when Gilbert receives a note threatening him with the death of Juliana, signed from "Dad". The handwriting is first identified as feminine and similar to that of [[Merinth Tharrows|The Man]], a post identifies the handwriting as close to that of Ferox.
- When he went unconscious, Gilbert received a vision of [[Virelya]]. They told Gilbert that his father was their chosen. As the leader of [[Qavrud]], this was presumed to be a reference to Ferox. Though Cyrena would be older and thus the heir, the patriarchal culture of Qavrud would cause her to be overlooked.
- Upon freeing Ferox from possession by the Emberheart, he told the party whilst under the effect of a truth circle, that he was in Qavrud at the time Gilbert would have been conceived, and has never had diamond nipple piercings.
### [[Kaedric Thal]]
- The party heard tell that Ferox had a twin brother who lost his son a long time ago.
- When Ferox revealed he would have been in Qavrud at the time of his conception, he also revealed that at the same time Kaedric was exploring the mainland of Thania.
- Kaedric also is said to bear a resemblance to [[Father Aldren]], a priest within the [[Motherhood of Vel'Enar]]. Though Aldren predates Kaedric by centuries, Dawn revealed that Aldren did indeed have diamond nipple piercings at one time.
## Juliana's Suspects
Juliana told the party that she had three suspects as to who could be Gilbert's father:
- An Elf with diamond nipple and a pearl penis tip piercings. The party has unequivocally decided that this candidate must be the father.
- Another Elf Juliana suspects was said to be very talented with ropes.
- Juliana also remembers a human who was good with his hips as another possibility.
