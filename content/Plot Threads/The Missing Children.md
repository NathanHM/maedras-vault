
## Involvement
- The party first heard about the missing children when [[Evangaline]] was kidnapped. ([[Session 01]])
- She was the 12th child to go missing in 12 weeks.
- The party rescued Evangaline unharmed. ([[Session 03]])
- Additionally [[Lady Aife Sweet]]'s son was kidnapped, but returned.
- Laura McMills' son and Mr White's granddaughter were also taken.
- Rumours said that the [[Motherhood of Vel'Enar]] was involved in the kidnappings. All of the kidnapped children studied under one of the Mothers. ([[Session 04]])
- Suspicion fell on [[High Lady Dusk]].
- The children claim to remember being fed something very sweet, like molasses, before passing out when they were kidnapped. They were then asked to pass a test by [[The Eternal Ember Cult|a group of paladins wearing blue and yellow sashes.]]
- Lord Farquad was found to have placed an order for Molasses. ([[Session 09]])
- The children woke up some time later with no further memories of the incident.
- Some of the children came back without the ability to talk ([[Session 07 08]])
- McMills' son Rupert reported that he saw three paladins in blue, a green flower, heard the word Alithi, four paladins in yellow, and one [[Vladimir Vladishkov|blue-clad man]] with a sword taller than himself. The party later learned the area he saw was the cult barracks within [[Lucanius]].
- Rupert reported no passage of time, but was gone for weeks. Potentially the children are also losing time to [[Transport Circles]].
- The cult kidnapped Evangaline again, along with [[High Lady Dawn]]. High Lady Dawn escaped, but Evangaline has not been seen since. 
- The party meet [[Father Ellric]] who confirms to them that the kidnappings are sacrifices to fuel [[The Emberheart]]. ([[Session 14]]) Later interrogating [[Vladimir Vladishkov|Vlad]], the sacrifice is of the souls of the children, so the children that have already been taken have lost their souls but they may be recovered as they will be sacrificed all at once. ([[Session 29]])
- A cultist reveals that the plan is to steal exactly 48 children. ([[Session 15]])
- The cultist target children for various attributes such as strength, intelligence, cunning.
- Whilst kidnapped, Dawn overheard that Dusk had sanctioned the kidnappings.
- The party learned from a vision that [[Dayne Greenway]] was the next target of the cult, as earlier warned by Dawn. ([[Session 07 08]], [[Session 19]])
- Talking to Vlad reveals that [[Fizziwinkle]], [[Lord Farquad]] and [[Bilbo Frankenfert]] were all involved with the kidnappings. ([[Session 20 21]])
- The party interfered with the cults plans and prevented Dayne from being taken during the peace summit.

## Goals
- Rescue Evangaline.
- Rescue the rest of the taken children.
- Discover the exact nature of the ritual being undertaken.
- Return the taken children to their former state, rescue their souls.
- Find and stop Fizziwinkle and Farquad, the last known active participants in the kidnappings.
