- They reunite with the [[Talis Greenway|Greenways]], who are relieved to see that [[High Lady Dawn|Dawn]] is alright, and they agree that the party will communicate all they have learned to them later.

- The party attempts to cash in their favour with [[Lord Regent Titus]] to get into the peace summit, but he refuses. The party then instead challenge him to an arm-wrestle with Wynona. Winning the contest and smashing Titus through the table, the party wins the right to serve as his guard during the peace talks, an additional 5000 gold and Titus's unwanted affection for Wynona.
- As the party proceeds, Lady Dusk eventually bids the party and Dawn adieu, and goes off to join her date for the evening, [[Lord High Priest Ferox Thal]]

# The Bard's Song
🌑 Verse 1  
	In fields where night-lilies dare not bloom,  
	And roots curl ‘neath the ash-blown tomb,  
	There danced a child with fire for breath,  
	Who laughed at chains and whispered death.
🌘 Verse 2  
	A cradle lies in forest deep,  
	Where silence sings and branches weep.  
	The oldest oath, now cracked and worn,  
	Shall bleed again when flame is born.
🌕 Chorus  
	So spin, O wheel, of fire and vine,  
	The ancient heart beats down the line.  
	When circles blaze and smoke is crowned,  
	The soul once lost shall rise unbound.
🌒 Verse 3  
	Three masks walk where none should tread,  
	One walks for gold, one walks for dread,  
	The third walks on with guilt in hand—  
	The ash-child knows and makes its stand.
🌑 Verse 4  
	A pyre lit in garden grand,  
	With hollow kings on borrowed land.  
	The blood of kin, the laugh of flame,  
	The stone remembers all their names.
🌕 Final Chorus  
	So drink and dance, ye noble fools,  
	While servants sharpen sacred tools.  
	The blaze is near, the spark's been fed—  
	The heart once cracked will burn instead.

# The Ballad of The Burning Crown

🎶 The Ballad of the Burning Crown
Verse I – The Crown Forged
	Upon the anvil of fire and stone,
	The smiths of old set a king on the throne.
	In gold and flame, the crown was bound,
	A kingdom's fate in its circlet found.
Chorus
	🔥 O Burning Crown, hold fast the flame,
	Through war, through ash, through endless name.
	From smoke to sky, from ash to ground,
	All kneel before the Burning Crown. 🔥
Verse II – The Ashen Court
	The court stood proud, the banners high,
	But shadow stirred where sparks could lie.
	A whispered oath, a pyre's breath,
	The Crown would burn to conquer death.
Chorus
	🔥 O Burning Crown, hold fast the flame,
	Through war, through ash, through endless name.
	From smoke to sky, from ash to ground,
	All kneel before the Burning Crown. 🔥
Verse III – The King's Fall
	The king was ash, the throne was bare,
	The Crown still burned in empty air.
	Those who seek its ember's light,
	Will trade the day to lose the night.
Final Chorus (Drawn out, ominous)
	🔥 O Burning Crown, hold fast the flame,
	Through war, through ash, through endless name.
	From smoke to sky, from ash to ground,
	All kneel before the Burning Crown…
	…All kneel before the Burning Crown. 🔥
