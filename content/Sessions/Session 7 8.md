The last two sessions were spent investigating the haunting at the de Beauvoir home. You started by taking the suspected poison to High Lady Dawn to confirm what it does and if she found traces on Nonna’s body 2 months ago. You learned that it was a poison that mimics sleep but does cause someone to die and is indeed produced by Qavrud. She reassured you that if something were to happen with the ghost, she would have the ability to heal you. You also acquired a bottle of holy water to use on the ghost and confirmed that salt would trap her. 
The party also spoke with High Lady Dawn about what happened at the ball to check in and see if anything was being done. She told you that they suspect Alithi was behind the job, and that recently there was a rumor that High Leader Talis’ son Dinn would be the next target so that the peace treaty wouldn’t go through. She told you that should things progress, you would be called upon. 
Seeing as the party had time until sunset when the ghost would appear, you decided to check in with McMills’ son and Mr. White’s granddaughter to see how they fared after the kidnapping. You first checked in with the McMills son and learned that he was on the boat with his father, but the mother assured you that they would call upon you in the morning once they were back ashore. With the White’s you spoke with the granddaughter who told you that she remembered being offered offly sweet tea and was told that she needed to pass a test by the paladins. She’s not sure what happened next, but woke up in the graveyard about 4 weeks ago. Jorvic also took the time to order some new boots which would be ready in 3 days.
As it was nearing night, you went back to the de Beauvoir house and prepared to catch a ghost. This involved the children in the red sitting room surrounded in nearly complete salt circles with one around the room as a whole. As night neared, Nonna’s ghost appeared and refused to be reasoned with. After some quick footwork by the party, you managed to trap Nonna in a salt circle and convince her to talk. She agrees to leave and not possess the children if Lady Bly is brought to justice and her death is avenged. You hailed a carrier child to deliver a letter to both High Lday Dawn and the local Sheriff asking for their assistance. The party managed to convince Sheriff Francois to wait until morning so you could get paid from Lady Bly. After a short rest, you woke to receive your reward of 25 gold from Lady Bly, and as Max put cuffs on her, a card appeared which led you to discover that there is something else important hidden at another location. Lady Bly was then quickly arrested and the search for the Lord began. 
The party decided to investigate what they assumed was her office above the butchers. Thanks to some music from Gilbert, Max was able to unpick the lock with no one seeing. Investigating the office, you found a ledger book written in code and a secret hollowed out book that on the outside says it is about Qavrud religions but inside is about Alithi religions. At the start, there were some trade deals made for Qavrud plants in exchange for hides but after a deeper investigation you noticed some code written in bastardised old Qavrudian, with the symbols you recognise as lavender, trade, mystery and death. You presented the documents found to the Sheriff and received a reward of 20sp each.
The session ended with the party checking back in at the tavern, and seeing a note from the McMills. You decided to pay them a visit and discovered that the son no longer speaks since being kidnapped. After some charades and poorly spelt words, you learned that he went to another place with his spirit not in his body and saw three paladins in blue on the otherside. There was also a green flower, heard the word Alithi, four paladins in yellow, and one of the men in blue had a big sword that was taller than him.
