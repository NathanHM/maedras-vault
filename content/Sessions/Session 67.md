- After spending some time in the [[Fey|Feywild]], [[Max]] returns to the group and reunites with them in the Temple of Four Lights.
- [[Uta'ah]] speaks with a cleric, asking where he can find [[Lady Mid]] so that she can help protect [[High Lady Dawn]] when the party move forward. The cleric suggests that Mid might not be the best positioned for this job, as her primary role in the religion resolves around lambing, but Uta’ah insists. He also asks who the god of intelligence is in the church of [[Vel'Enar]], so that he may visit their shrine.
- [[Cyrena]] and [[Chamae]] speak with Dawn, and investigate her shrine. There they notice that the shrine is decorated with bronze leaves, decorated with names that rapidly switch. One of the leaves bears no name, just a date in the near future, the 21st of Twilightmere. 
- They speak with an older lady who has also noticed the dimmed violet flame, and tells the party she believes the light is dimmed due to a disturbance in the worship in that section of the church, Dusk’s section. She speaks to Dawn on the insistence of Chamae, sharing her beliefs.
- Max arrives at the temple, and joins Uta’ah at the shrine to [[Ceylira]]. They briefly discuss what has happened to the party since they last saw each other, but Max leaves Uta’ah when they realise he wants to make a prayer in privacy.
- Uta’ah leaves an offering to Ceylira, a shiny stone he was gifted by a child after a performance he gave on the day he first met the party. He prays that she might help him make better choices.
- Chamae and Cyrena speak with a stressed cleric to ask about the leaves. She tells them that the leaves show the passage of souls between life and death. They seem to be strained right now, with too many passing through to death, and too few returning to life. The leaf with the fixed date has been stuck for a while now, and there is a rumour that the [[Kalamari|octopi]] will end the world on that date.
- Max reunites with Cyrena and Chamae, and the three look back towards the older woman who noticed the dimmed flame. After speaking with her, Max detects a soot-like taste, and Cyrena notices her eyes are amber, similar to the golden eyes of [[Lord High Priest Ferox Thal]] when he was possessed by [[The Emberheart]].
- Fetching Uta’ah, they tell Dawn that they suspect everyone might be in danger. She coordinates her clerics to start evacuating the temple whilst Uta’ah and Cyrena go to confront the old woman. On the way, Uta‘ah grabs the cleric Cyrena and Chamae spoke with, and demands she get Dawn out of here too.
- Speaking with the older woman, she identifies herself as [[Iotrareno]] The pair try to convince her to accept their blessings, in an attempt to cast their combined magics on her and potentially protect her from possession, but she refuses their blessing unless they take hers first. Unable to convince her, tensions rise, and Uta’ah swaps tact, and instead claims he is [[Maedra Lurith]] and that since he is an arch-fey she must keep her end of the bargain if he agrees to accept her blessing. She grows angered by the suggestion he is Maedra, and begins to grow in size.
- Cyrena coats her hand in Black Iron powder, causing Iotrareno to recoil in pain. Uta’ah and Cyrena make one final attempt to force their magic on her, but she dodges their attacks.
- Before conflict can begin, Max slips off to the altar of Ceylira, the goddess who gave them their [[Divine Touch]], having discovered their conenction in the Feywild. A prayer to them results in a time stop in which Max lays cards from their Tarot deck around the temple, banishing Iotrareno and a few others within the circle once time resumes.
- The party question how Max discovered this connection, and Max reveals that they spent some days in the Feywild, investigating their connection to their deity, their new found divinatory abilities, and developing a bond with their new familiar. Uta’ah is slightly hurt that Max would depart from the party for days without telling them so that they may learn in isolation.
- Uta’ah heads to the backroom of the temple to check if Dawn is okay, where he sees her being comforted by a young Halfling woman he realises is in fact Lady Mid.
- Returning to the party, Uta’ah climbs the statue of Vel’Enar to investigate the unlit lamp it carries, which the party believe can be lit when Vel’Enar chooses.
- Questioning Max on how they discovered their link to their patron god, Max suggests that the Temple with its altars to each god would be a good place for them each to uncover their connections. As he begins to ponder this, Uta’ah is struck with a fireball as a [[Divine Consequence]], and falls to the ground.
