- With the legendary bathtub chariot now patented, and the peace talks wrapped, the party reunite with [[High Lady Dawn]] to determine the outcome of the peace summit.
- Joining Dawn snd [[Talis Greenway]] in the Alithi quarters, the party first check up on Dawn’s wellbeing. Though she still seems fatigued and troubled by the consequences of her revivification of [[Uta'ah]], she does seem certain that she made the correct decision.
- She and Talis tell the party that the four nations have agreed to a commitment of 10 years of sustained peace efforts in the face of the threat of [[The Eternal Ember Cult]].
- Dawn intends to miss out on the closing celebrations, instead heading to the Temple of Four Lights, an old temple of [[Vel'Enar]] that still stands in [[Lucanius]]. She agrees to take guards with her to ensure her safety, and the party will join her later after the celebrations.
- The party move to the party where they find a large tower of drinks and plenty of gambling tables. [[Cyrena]] and [[Chamae]] make a bet about how many drinks Uta’ah will be able to take from the tower before knocking down drinks. Uta’ah bets all his money that he will not knock over a single drink, but immediately causes a few to fall on his first trip, taking two for himself and one for everyone else.
- [[Gilbert]] quickly moves to the tables, where he finds [[Maedra Lurith]]. Cyrena and Uta’ah join the two in the game, whilst Chamae watches. Uta'ah borrows money from his friends and competitors at the table to match the pot.
- Maedra and Cyrena end the first game in a tie, whilst the second game is won by Maedra outright, even after the groups continuous failed attempts to cheat. Gilbert makes an additional bet against maedra, wagering all her money against all of his. Maedra escalates the deal to include one favour, though Gilbert and Cyrena agree on the stipulation the favour not go against any of the current party’s morals. Maedra wins the bet, leaving Gilbert penniless and with a pending IOU.
- After many drinks, with Uta’ah having twice as many as everyone else, Uta’ah and Cyrena take to the dance floor with an incredible performance to the music of Layla Swing. 
- Chamae seeks out [[Lady Visyra]], the drunk woman the party met at the opening celebrations. With help from Cyrena and Gilbert, Chamae sells all of the wine the party found, except one of the aged barrels. Visyra provides the party with a sending stone to contact her and sell any more wine they come across.
- Uta’ah, after drunkenly knocking over the entire tower of drinks, seeks out Maedra so that he may fulfil his end of his bargain with her, to tell her his greatest secret. Uta’ah tells Maedra that he feels things might be better if Dawn hadn’t brought him back, and he had remained dead, fearing that he keeps hurting people, namely Dawn and [[Lera Vane]], with his stupid choices. He then returns to the dance floor.
- Cyrena seeks out Maedra to keep her part of the deal, and lights up with her. They discuss Gilber’s favour, and Cyrena tries to ensure that the favour will not be anything too dramatic. Maedra then tell Cyrena some of what Uta’ah told her, and Cyrena goes to speak with him.
