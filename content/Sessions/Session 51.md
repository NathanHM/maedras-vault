- In the wake of the battle, the party takes this temporary reprieve to work the room.
- [[Max]] manages to convince the civilian uprising to leave and set down their arms for now.
- Confronting [[High Lady Dusk|Dusk]] for her actions, she claims that she acted only to prevent the escalation of conflict, but still distrustful of her, the party has her gagged and left in the care of [[High Lady Dawn]].
- At [[Chamae]]'s behest, [[Uta'ah]] tries to break the shrunken table, but is unable to do so. [[Cyrena]] decides to take action and tries to scratch one of the symbols away, only for the entire [[Transport Circles|circle]] to disappear.
- Speaking to [[Lord Votrin|Votrin]] and [[Lord Regent Titus|Titus]], the party learn there is a suspected link between [[Rellis Torne]], the leader of the mercenary guild and the Black Blades, and Merinth Tharrows. The pair have been running raids on caravans on the roads, but notably not near the [[Ashenhold]]. Rellis has not been paying his taxes, which Voltrin finds offensive. Rellis is the one who has been offering contracts to protect "entertainers".
- Votrin expands on the information he has received from his scholars. He has heard of the Flamelord and the Hour of Flame, which the party suspect to be [[the Emberheart]] by a different name. The research also talks about the [[Ashen Hourglass]], a time trap that seems to be the resevior for the stolen time from the transport circles. Breaking this hourglass may cause all the time to be released and passed through at once.
- [[Gilbert]] talks to [[Vaela]] and interrogates her on her relationship with [[Lord High Priest Ferox Thal|Ferox]]. She denies that the two have an intimate relationship, and seems to be being truthful. She was recruited by some priests of [[Virelya]] years ago, and she doesn't appear to have been specifically targeted. She has no knowledge about any link between Ferox and the Emberheart, and is a true follower of Virelya. She claims to have once met Ferox's brother, [[Kaedric Thal|Kaedric]], as he greets people at the docks when they first arrive.
- Before Ferox awakens, Uta'ah has Titus summon the mages who established the anti-magic fields, so that they may place a temporary field around him. The head mage does not arrive.
- As Ferox awakens, the party notice that his eyes have shifted from gold to a forest green, indicating he is no longer possessed by the Emberheart. Ferox does not know what is going on, and appears to have no memories of the last six years.
- When interrogated about his brother, Ferox claims that Kaedric died some 6 months ago from his perspective, placing it six and a half years ago in reality.
- Kaedric had travelled the world, visiting a [[Stormhaven|coastal town]] in [[Rulif]], a [[Verdant Pyre|decaying green ruin]] to the east, and some [[Ashenhold|dungeon or funeral graves]]. He returned to [[Qavrud]] claiming he found a [[Vath'ira|Vath'iran]] book on his travels.
- Ferox has [[Diamond Nips and a Pearl Tip - The Mystery of Cyrena and Gilbert's Father|no piercings]] and claims he would not have been travelling during the time at which Gilbert and Cyrena were conceived, suggesting Kaedric is the more likely candidate for their father.
- Ferox's predecessor, Samuel McMannis, was struggling with a drought in Qavrud. Kaedric was able to convince him to sacrifice himself in a burning ritual detailed in the Vath'iran book. When the ritual failed to fix things, Ferox was expected to be sacrificed next. Kaedric took his place, convincing everyone that Ferox would be required to lead Qavrud. After this second sacrifice, the draught finally ended.
- To Ferox's knowledge, Kaedric died in that event, but Vaela and Ferox's guards insist that Kaedric has been alive these past six years.
