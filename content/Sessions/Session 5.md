Last week, the party first decided to rescue poor Rufus’ pet giant crab as it was just past Lord Farquad's house. You decided to do some recon as you past the manor (picture to come) and noticed that on the grounds were two landscappers. Seeing that the property was surrounded by 7ft stone fences and a wrought iron front gate, you decided to come back later. 
Once you arrived at the beach, Gilbert was quick to speak with the local crabs. Here you learned that the big one was stolen by the sky demons (giant eagles) who have been terrorizing the local area. Sending an invisible Jorvic to investigate their nest, the party decided to convince the eagles to leave as they spoke common. Once the eagles fled with their eggs, you quickly recovered the crab and returned her home. You were rewarded by Rufus and his dad Daniel 50gp each. Chamae warned the Lochs that while the eagles were no longer at the beach, they might still terrorize the town.
You decided to visit some shops as Jorvic wanted an extra Javalin. The party visited Wander o Wonder, a magical shop, where Gilbert’s future was read and the party was offered the chance to buy a common magical item. Seeing that it was still day, you went to check on Evangaline, who was more energetic than normal and counting rather poorly in Elvish, which High Lady Dawn taught her for funeral rights. Here you learned that children interested in joining the motherhood of Vel’enar take a language aptitude test. The only details she was able to provide about the night of her kidnapping was that the tea was very sweet, almost molasses tasting. 
This led to a decision to visit Lady Aife Sweet’s children Oliver and Olivia to see how they were. Both children were taught Sylvan by Lady Mid to aid in the lambing rights. Oliver revealed that he was jealous of his sister and through your insight, you think he failed the test for progression. He provided the names of who he thought were the other top students under High Lady Dusk and Lady Night, Erica and someone else, respectively. He also told you that Laura McMills’ son was studying under High Lady Dusk (undercommon) while Mr White the cobbler’s daughter was studying under Lady Night (primordial).
