- As the party prepare to face off with [[Merinth Tharrows|The Man]] and the Basilisk, they decide to investigate [[Vladimir Vladishkov|Vlad]]'s sword as a potential weapon for the fight. A [[Vath'ira|Vath'iran]] inscription on the blade reads "Ember Knights of Valour Strong".
- The group decide that [[Cyrena]] can make the most use of the blade, but will require attunement. Prioritising the Pass Without Trace spell over attunement, the group decide to act now and attune later. [[Chamae]] inks Cyrena with magical armour, [[Uta'ah]] activates his own tattoo and [[Gilbert]] provides Bardic Inspiration as the party ready themselves. They discuss strategy for fighting the Basilisk, choosing to keep lighting in the room whilst trying to use [[Wynona]]'s axe, enchanted by Chamae, to blind the creature.
- Approaching the cistern where the [[Myconids]] say the Basilisk will be found, Gilbert spots two humanoid shadows. [[Max]] takes the lead as the group line up.
- Within the cistern, a few deceased ogres are found, with a [[Fey]] woman sitting upon a chest. The woman does not precisely match the appearance of anyone the party has met so far, but does resemble Merinth Tharrows. 
- She and Max talk to the best of their ability, whilst both try to come across as mysterious as they can. The woman does reveal that she was hired to challenge the party.
- With the group's backing, Max attacks the Fey woman, who animates the dead ogres before vanishing.
- The group enter the cistern and start fighting the ogres, and see the basilisk begin to emerge from the water.
- As Gilbert enters, he decides to throw one of his mystery potions at the creature. This angers the group when the first potion he throws appears to provide the Basilisk with additional health. Doubling down, Gilbert throws another potion. The second potion miraculously teleports the Basilisk back to the Green Soldier tavern in [[Kilsa]] where Gilbert first found them.
- With the primary threat dealt with, the group shift focus to the ogres. Cyrena realises she can electrocute the water in the cistern with her ring, so the party push all the ogres into the water, chipping away at them with ranged attacks.
- The ogres prove to be durable and take some time to drop. As the party works on this, Wynona and Chamae approach the chest. Burned by a Mimic before, Chamae attempts a psychic attack on the chest, and discovers that it is indeed a Mimic.
- The session ends with one ogre clinging on in the water and the party shifting focus to the Mimic, debating whether to dunk the chest or preserve the treasure potentially within.
