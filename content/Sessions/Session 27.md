Bilbo explains that the orbs are used as a means of communication between cult members and that Oracles use them to scry and observe through them, and can even be used as a teleport anchor (? double check this info). Oracles have their eyes burnt out in the process. Orbs contain part of the Emberheart’s corporeal form and the cult is supposed to spread them all over the world.

It’s determined that she likely has been blessed by Thalana (The Veiled Dreamer, goddess of Nights & Secrets). The flames within the orbs are not extinguishable by any means. The party are now in possession of two orbs, which makes some members nervous.

The Burning Dawn - the cult’s belief that a rapture day will come when the Emberheart is reborn and the world burns. They think they will be saved (sad trombone).

Bilbo thinks that Lord Ferox and Kaedric Thall may be twin brothers, and that Ferox has a missing son. When asked about Farquad, Bilbo reveals that Farquad is on poor terms with his sister because the matriarchal society left him with nothing and her with everything. Chamae recognises the description of the sister to match a previous client, Merenth Tharrows. It’s correctly deduced that Merenth Tharrows is The Man.

Verdant Pyre (Rulif) - cult activity near Wynona’s home - mushroom people, very dangerous area. Need more info here.

Bilbo falls asleep and Uta'ah almost kicks him to death - a worthy fate for such slime, had the party not promised him to Siobhan.
