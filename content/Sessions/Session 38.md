- The party then head off to [[Lucanius#Southern Districts|Honi]] to meet with a priestess who claims there have been [[Rumours from Rexius#Echoes in the Graveyard – Graveyard, Honi|grave robbings]] in the [[Lucanius#Notable Buildings & Locations#South Districts|Old Graveyard of Saint Immurel]].
- On the way, the party stop for a group portrait, and spot a series of wanted posters featuring mischaracterized versions of the group. [[Wynona]] has a human face, [[Gilbert]] has a massive nose, [[Max]] is in their twinky paladin form but with a bowl cut, [[Cyrena]] has been given bulbous eyes, [[Uta'ah]] has ears, and [[Chamae]] and [[Chamae#Chamae's Constructs|Manny]] have been conflated into one.
- Uta'ah pulls down the posters and the party proceeds.
- The party arrives at the Chapel of [[Church of The Eternal Vine|The Eternal Vine]], where they meet with a nun, Mother [[Barbarastreisand]]. She is serving a punishment of squeezing grapes, as she has been looking into the grave robbings which the fathers at the church deny are occurring.
- Through talking with Barbarastreisand, the party discover that most of the nuns at the chapel are in fact indentured servants, kept against their will.
- As Gilbert and Uta'ah get caught up in the grape squeezing, Max and Cyrena sneak upstairs to look around. Investigating the offices, they find correspondence between the [[Father Ophius]] of the church and Father Hamlet, part of the leadership of the Eternal Vine. The fathers are concerned about [[Halric Dain]] pushing for modernisation, increased industrialisation and militarization, and traditional values getting lost on the way. There is a letter praising Ophius for bringing enlightenment to the people of the docks.
- In a safe, they find the contracts for Barbarastreisand and the other nuns, along with a bloody knife, a map of the graves, and stones that will make noise at night.
- They take the contents of the safe, and forge evidence to frame [[Cult of the Eternal Ember|the cult]] for the theft. Returning downstairs, they inform Barbarastreisand that she and the other nuns are free, and they should leave tonight and take refuge at the [[derelict vineyard]] where the party arrived in [[Alithi]].
- The party then investigates the graveyard. They find some Gravegrasp, which they struggle to harvest without taking psychic damage. Cyrena attempts to drown out the effects of the Graveyard by smoking some of the parties' own weed.
