The party arrives in a dark cellar full of dusty old wine casks. A great deal of super strength ancient wine is appropriated, including an entire barrel that Uta'ah is carrying around drinking from like a cat. Chamae fills Lifty with several barrels, stating that she can trade them or start a travelling tavern - an idea which Uta'ah seems fully invested in. The building appears to be an old, derelict vineyard with a teleportation circle in the basement.

There are wolves in the area - Uta'ah theorises that him scenting the area should scare off animals who might smell a dragon. Uta'ah chooses a spot to mark and successfully slips and falls in his own piss. 

A white dragon appears, attracted by the scent of another dragon and reveals herself as Shiverwatcher - or Siobhan, when in human form. A young dragon still looking to build her hoard, Siobhan attempts to entice Uta'ah several times and seems interested in bargaining. The party offers her (the still unconscious) Bilbo in exchange for her help, determining that some time has passed since entering the transport circle and arriving [how much time has passed?], and that they are currently near the capital city. The vineyard where Gilbert's mother, Juliana is being held is on the way. The party decides to save Juliana first then go to the capital for the Peace Summit. [how many days until the summit?] Siobhan agrees to fly the party to the vineyard and assist in the battle after extracting some more promises from the party.

Siobhan mentions that her sisters also frequent the area and that she has made the journey to the city on foot only a few times. She and her sisters can often be found at the White Star Coffee Shop in the city where they hunt.
