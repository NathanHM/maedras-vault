- A few years on from [[The Peace Summit]], the party has given up on their quest to save the world and opened their own tavern, Suckers. [[Gilbert]] has gained weight, grown a beard and become celibate. [[Wynona]] runs brutal adventurer training courses. [[Uta'ah]] serves as the eye-candy bartender. [[Max]] has definitely gone through major changes, though no one can quite see what has changed.
- Three weeks on from the annual holiday of Giftmas, a gunslinging Aarakocra named [[Blip]] arrives carrying a letter from the tavern's primary investor [[Maedra Lurith]].
- Since Giftmas, Uta'ah has been missing, and Maedra is worried about how this may affect profits. She instructs the party to go and find him with the aid of Blip.
- Recalling that Uta'ah sought to give the party the "greatest Giftmas gift", the party contact [[Siobhan]] to see if she has any knowledge of where Uta'ah may be.
- Siobhan reveals that Uta'ah spoke about the town of Giftmas where he might find this great gift.
- Setting out via the now commodified transport circles, the group reach the town of Giftmas in the Tayan mountains.
- There they encounter a single guard, who identifies Wynona as the Easter Bunny, Gilbert as a St. Patrick's day leprechaun, Blip as the American spirit of the Fourth of July, and Max as the aspect of Pride month. 
- Fearing these rival holidays are intending to invade the territory of Giftmas, the guard is reluctant to help, but with the entire town largely drained in the wake of Giftmas, he quickly gives in.
- He reveals that Father Giftmas is missing, having wandered into the mountains. There have been many Father Giftmases over the centuries, with the title passing on via the Crown of Father Giftmas. If they live long enough, they eventually wander into the mountains, never to be seen again. and the Crown eventually returns to the town.
- He also reveals that a Dragonborn did arrive in the town on the day of Giftmas, but was arrested due to fears he was the Dragon of Chinese New Year. He has since escaped, but first broke into the library.
- Following Uta'ah's trail to the library, they find he read a (children's picture) book on the origins of the Crown of Father Giftmas. The Crown was forged by two brothers. One became the first Father Giftmas, whilst the other paid the price for the magic of the crown. He was banished into the mountains and has since been referred to as the Beast.
- Wynona identifies the mountain in the picture book as one close by the town. Surmising that must be where Uta'ah went, the party set out after him. Braving the harsh weather, they eventually reach a cave high in the mountains with an abandoned sleigh out front.
- Delving into the cavern, they discover the bones of beasts and men litter the ground. Entering a main chamber, they see the eviscerated corpse of Father Giftmas on a ritualist pedestal, with the Crown floating above his corpse. 
- Spotting something moving high above on the ceiling, the party stay back whilst Gilbert sends an unseen servant to retrieve the crown. Launching from the shadows, the servant is attacked by a humanoid creature coated in green hair with cruel animalistic eyes. The party recognise this monster as The Gronch.
- The party leap into combat. Gilbert attempts to communicate with the beast, and Max attempts to magically charm it, but their attempts are thwarted as the Gronch's mind is too animalistic.
- Wynona and Blip take the fight to the monster, forcing it to retreat and scale the walls. The Gronch spews venom at the party, nearly bringing Gilbert down immediately.
- As Wynona can't reach the Gronch high on the wall, she instead takes the Crown. Growing a large white beard from her furry face, she becomes the first Mother Giftmas. The Gronch turns its attention to her with a renewed hatred, and returns to the ground.
- Combined attacks from Blip and Max manage to fell the beast. As the creature gives its last strangled breath, Uta'ah finally arrives.
- He reveals that he intentionally baited the party into following him here so that they may take the Crown and slay the Gronch. With the Crown now in their hands they can use it to draw more patrons to their tavern, thus giving the party the greatest gift of all; the joy of Capitalism.
