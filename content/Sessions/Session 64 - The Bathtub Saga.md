- [[Uta'ah]], [[Cyrena]] and [[Chamae]] find themselves in the Alithi quarters with a divine quest; find a bathtub worthy of carrying the octopi out to the sea.
- The group debate stealing one of the bathtubs from either the staff quarters or one of the quarters housing the diplomats. Wishing to avoid incurring more ire from the nations leaders or the staff, they decide instead to ply Cyrena’s woodworking and Chamae’s metalwork to creating their own bathtub.
- In order to get the wood for the bathtub, the group go to the courtyard where they find three trees, an apple tree, a cherry tree and a large oak tree. Speaking to a nearby guard, they learn that these trees are sacred as the seeds were brought over from Alithi proper.
- Forgetting their prior commitment to not incur the wrath of the leaders, the group decide to cut down the oak tree. Once the guards have cleared from the courtyard, Cyrena cast silence whilst she and Uta’ah cut down the tree. 
- After a sweaty montage, the tree silentlfy falls. One group of guards passes by without noticing, but a second group arrive and start questioning the party on their actions. A flexing Uta’ah convinces the guards to agree to keep the incident quiet, on the condition that they plant a new tree. Taking one of the acorns from the tree, Chamae uses Druidcraft to grow a new tree, larger than the old one.
- Prying a decorative railing loose for the metal, the group return to the Alithi quarters. Cyrena etches the bathtub with designs of [[Kalamari]], [[Paella]] and [[Nuloran]]. To further enrich the design, the party go to Titus quarters to steal some precious stones.
- Whilst in Titus quarters, Cyrena and Chamae investigate some of the letters they missed last time they were there. Reading Titus correspondence, they find he has been communicating with his cousin, the leader of Alithi proper. Titus had been pushing to be made a true ruler of the Alithi province in Thania. The correspondence shows that his cousin reminded Titus of his place, and refused his request.
- Investigating Titus’ vanity mirror, Chamae finds a necklace that she identifies as the Siren necklace. She swipes the necklace, though leaves behind a jewellery box that takes her fancy, which Uta’ah notices and steals to gift to her later.
- Returning to the construction of the tub, the party decide to affix a front car to the tub so that [[Lera Vane]] may ride along with the octopi, freed from her position as a familiar but still serving as their translator.
- After Uta’ah and Chamae fail to create a combustion engine, they affix reins so that the tub may be pulled by Rain, Bow, Sun and Shine, the horses brought to the town by the party.
- Chamae affixes the bed canopy to the bathtub to hide the octopi as they travel, whilst Uta’ah sews some of the teal scales he shed whilst cutting down the tree into a cushion for Lera, so that she may remember him.
- After Chamae determines that she can recreate the gasoline if the party find the correct ingredients, they decide to use some of the gasoline she has secreted so far to create a flame trap affixed to the back of the tub. Additionally a crossbow is attached to the front, and retractable spikes are inserted in the wheels.
- A group of onlookers gather and wonder at the creation of the bathtub chariot. As the design is finalised, Uta’ah rushes off to file a patent, and immediately three more of the bath chariots are comissioned.
- As he returns to the palace, the peace talks reach their conclusion.
