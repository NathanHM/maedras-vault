- After [[Session 58|recent successes]] in dismantling [[The Eternal Vine]] via spreading stories, the party has decided to bring the fight to [[The Eternal Ember Cult]] by getting the word out about the cult’s machinations.
- To achieve this, the party will put on a play recounting their adventures and fights with the cults.
- [[Uta'ah]] has been squirrelled away developing the play for the past few weeks, and the day has arrived. The party go to the Sunmore Amphitheatre where they find a packed crowd and bustling production.
- Heading backstage, they meet a frayed [[Cobalt]]. Harassed by a tyrannical Uta’ah, he informs the party that they will be starring in the play.
- After a brief skirmish between Uta’ah and [[Gilbert]] over the lack of a script, the party are hurried to the wings and dress to go on stage.
- [[High Lady Dawn]] acts as the narrator for the play. She introduces the first act, set three years in the past at the wedding of Princess [[Evangaline]]. The party are introduced as guests of honour at the ball, with [[Cyrena]] playing [[Wynona]], Manny stepping into the role of [[Max]], and Cobalt portraying Gilbert, whilst Gilbert fills the role of [[Merinth Tharrows|The Man]].
- The party notice a reviewer in the crowd. A failed attempt to cast Suggestion by Gilbert sets the play off on a poor note. When the scene turns to The Man kidnapping Evangaline, Gilbert accidentally suplexes the actress and forgets how to speak Common. A quick bit of magic from [[Chamae]] allows Gilbert and the actress to slip off stage, closing the first act.
- Between acts, the party retreat backstage and meet a trauma-stricken Uta’ah. He is terrified that the opening night reviews will tarnish his career as a playwright before it has a chance to take off. As it turns out, he has also invested the party’s entire wealth into the production.
- Gilbert (magically) convinces Uta’ah to join the rest of the party onstage. Gilbert then spikes Cyrena’s food with a Mystery Potion, causing her to sprout a third arm. In retort, Cyrena electrocutes Gilbert with her ring.
- Uta’ah takes Cyrena aside whilst the others are brought back into position by Cobalt, now set to take over the role of Wynona.
- After a show-stopping musical number from Gilbert, the second act commences. The party find themselves hunting The Man in a ruined city. There they meet [[Kalamari]], the last of the alien Vath’irans. He commands the party to find and defeat the other last Vath’iran, [[The Emberheart]]. To aid in their quest, he lends them the aid of his twin children, Uta’ah and Cyrena.
- Going back stage, Uta’ah asks the rest of the party to aid him in coming up with Act 3, which was left largely open, before Act 4 which Uta’ah says will be a “tasteful” love scene between Uta’ah and [[Siobhan]].
- The party call upon [[Juliana]], watching from the audience, to play Siobhan in the love scene. Forbidden from playing himself in the scene by the bro code, Cyrena takes over the role of Uta’ah.
- The party set for the third act to be a recreation of their fights in [[Kilsa]] and the Vineyard. Uta’ah has one of the trolls he bought for the production wheeled to the other side of the stage. The party takes to the stage, and quickly dispose of the troll, who Uta’ah identifies as [[Vladimir Vladishkov|Vlad]]. Uta’ah reveals Vlad’s last words; The Emberheart killed Gilbert’s father.
- Before the act finishes, Gilbert is possessed by the need to piss. He hurries over to Cyrena and pisses on her. Rightfully and fairly, she attacks Gilbert with the flaming sword of Vlad, smiting him down. She brings back the lightning from her ring, leaving Gilbert an inch from death.
- As a shocked Juliana rushes onstage, Cyrena reluctantly heals Gilbert before he dies. Uta’ah turns to the audience, and says this is the story of how the part learned not to piss on people.
- After a brief intermission, in which Uta’ah hands over the position of director to Chamae, the cast return on stage for the tasteful love scene.
- Juliana and Cyrena take to the bed onstage, hidden behind a thin scree. Chamae makes projections of love hearts whilst Uta’ah and Gilbert recreate some Phil Collins music. A convincing performance from Juliana wins the hearts of the audience.
- The cast prepare backstage for Act 5, which Uta’ah promises will be an epic conclusion to the story. The players take to the stage and are seated at a long table for the Peace Summit.
- As the scene gets underway, [[Maedra Lurith|Maedra]] arrives on stage, portraying [[The Emberheart]]. Uta’ah confronts “the Emberheart” about killing Gilbert’s father. Maedra responds, that she did not kill his father, she is his father. The rest of the party turns this simple Star Wars riff into a confusing Spartacus bit, as they each claim to be Gilbert’s father.
- Kicking the scene up a notch, Maedra casts Power Word Kill, instantly killing Uta’ah.
- The party leap into combat, trying to avenge the death of Uta’ah. Maedra tolerates their attacks before being stunned by Chamae’s Psychic Scream.
- Speaking as the Emberheart, she vows revenge on the party, unless the people of the world can unite and rise up to stop her. 
- As the curtains draw, the party turn their attentions to the deceased Uta’ah. Before they have the chance to act, they are shocked by the arrival of the stunning [[Lady Mid]]. With an effortless look, she revivifies Uta’ah.
- Peering out into the crowd, the party look to see if they left a positive impact on the reviewer. After weighing up the performance, he seems displeased.
- Cutting forward a few years, the party die at the hands of The Emberheart as he burns the world. Uta’ah laments that they could have saved the world, if only the play had gone better.
