- As the party rest and recover, [[Chamae]] summons her new familiar,, [[Chamae#Chamae's Constructs#Shifty|Shifty]], a small lizard she sends to investigate the west wing.
- Climbing across the walls and roof, Shiny goes unnoticed as Chamae watches through its eyes. In the west wing they find a large library, but no indication of any [[Transport Circles|circle]]
- In an unoccupied room, Chamae does find a circle with a five point star in it, but quickly realises that this circle is not likely to operate, and is instead more likely a child's attempt to create something for attention
- The party venture into Trina Macbeth's office to delve down into big hole in ground.
- They make their way down into the darkness, relying on [[Cyrena]] and [[Gilbert]]'s Darkvision to provide guidance. Gilbert drinks one of his mysterious potions in the hopes it will provide some light, but instead it makes him unfathomably hideous for an hour. The party fail to notice any change.
- Quickly the tunnels break into three possible directions. To the west, Cyrena spots some dog shaped creatures making their way south. To the east, a creature bobs its head out of a cistern. To the north, the tunnel breaks into multiple directions, with the sound of shuffling echoing down the tunnels.
- Puttering her tracking abilities to use, Cyrena is able to determine that Trina went down the north tunnel, then off to the west. Following the path, the group find a room with four zombies and a body slumped in the corner. 
- Acting quickly, the party are able to attack before the zombies notice them, and dispatch of the undead before they have a chance to attack.
- Investigating the body in the corner, they find the deceased Trina Macbeth. Some investigation shows that this body has been here for weeks, though has been recently disturbed. A note is found on the body addressing the party, saying they will face off deeper in the tunnels. Additionally, [[Max]] manages to recover a pair of anti-magic handcuffs from the body.
- Pushing deeper into the sewers, the group reach another cross roads. To the east they spot a Corpse Flower. However Cyrena traces her quarry to the north, so the group bypass the flora for now.
- Eventually they reach another room, full of [[Myconids]]. Determining that they are not inherently hostile, the group approaches and find themselves connected to the creatures and each other via their fungal network.
- As the creatures emanate curiosity, [[Uta'ah]] responds with a sense of peace, allowing the two groups to communicate without violence. 
- Questioning of the Myconids shows that they have become lost from home, which they seem to consider to be the leader of their network. Questioned about the [[Verdant Pyre]], this seems to be where the Myconids are from, and where their leader should be. When Cyrena recalls the tale of [[Floral Myths and Legends of the Known Realms#1. The Thorned Crown|The Thorned Crown]], the Myconids respond with a sense that this story is familiar to them, a sacred tale though it is somewhat misconstrued.
- When questioned on [[The Eternal Ember Cult]], the Myconids emanate fear, and reveal that there appears to be a cult hotspot in the north-east sector of the sewers that they have been avoiding.
- From the Myconids, the group discover that Trina Macbeth has passed through recently, causing a sense of fear and danger from the Myconids. When prompted with mental images of [[Merinth Tharrows]], the Myconids are able to confirm that the two are one and the same.
- The Man appears to be laying in wait deeper in the tunnels, in a large cistern with a Basilisk. Cyrena is able to recall that the Basilisk is a large, many legged monstrosity. It is able to paralyze creatures if they can see it and it can see them. However, if the beast should be made to see its own reflection, it will target itself as a rival.
