The party decided to return to the Yellow Duck and quickly secured the lover room which had the biggest bath (3gp per day) and some food for Kalamari as well (1gp per day). 
At the Yellow Duck, you search for Lera, the woman you convinced to leave the cult, and manage to convince her that you are in fact the party who rescued here (even if you don’t look the same) and, after a heart-to-heart with Uta’ah, bring her up to speak with High Lady Dawn. In doing so, Gilbert manages to break a mirror and now had bad luck (so next 4 sessions, all Nat 2s count as Nat 1s). Dawn informs you of a vision she had while in reflection:
High Lady Dawn stands in a vast, twilight field where the sky is half-lit by a pale sun and half-shrouded in darkness. Silhouettes of the four nations rise in the distance, their borders bleeding into each other like ink in water. A Single Flame Flickers at her feet, small yet impossibly bright, casting a long shadow stretching toward the horizon. When she reaches out, the flame splits into four smaller flames, each moving in different directions toward one of the nations. One heads down, where it flares brightly, then sputters as if extinguished prematurely. One heads left, but flickers uncontrollably, casting violent shadows. One heads up, where ancient stones ignite, and symbols glow with burning script. And the final heads right, melting into the earth and causing the land to crack open, revealing a circle of shifting runes beneath the soil.
High Lady Dawn then sees a river of ash flowing through the nations, carrying withered branches. One branch is wrapped in blackened vines—the symbol of the Eternal Vine Monastery—while another burns with an ember that refuses to die, representing the Eternal Ember cult.
At the end of the vision, a figure cloaked in both fire and shadow stands at the center of an ancient circle. They place a token onto the circle, and the runes flare with burning light. As the circle activates, two doors appear: one leads to radiant light, the other to consuming darkness. The figure reaches for both at once—implying a choice that could reshape the future. Before the vision fades, High Lady Dawn hears a whisper of who she assumes is Vel’Enar saying: "Paths diverge. Flames flicker. Choose the branch that bends, or risk the forest burning. Time wanes. Seek the ember beneath stone, and the vine that strangles truth."
You awaken in a field of silver grass beneath a sky split into day and night, the sun and moon hanging opposite each other. The air is still, and every sound feels distant, as if you’re underwater. Ahead stands a vast tree with obsidian-black bark, its branches twisting upward like skeletal fingers. Half of its leaves are vibrant gold, while the other half are ashen and burned. Embers drift from the charred branches, carried by a wind you cannot feel. At the tree’s base lies a stone circle carved with shifting runes, pulsing faintly. Four tokens float above it, each bearing the crest of a different nation: A silver wolf for Rulif, a red blade for Qavrud, a golden spiral for Alithi, and a black flame for Tayan. One by one, the tokens fall onto the circle—but when the black flame token lands, the runes ignite with fire, and cracks split the ground, spreading like veins of molten rock.
The sky darkens, and from the horizon, a wave of ash and fire surges forward, swallowing the land. Out of the flame steps a figure cloaked in shadows, its face hidden. In its hand: an ember, glowing with an inviting warmth and in the other, a withered vine, curling and reaching out like a grasping hand. The figure holds them out toward you—but before you can choose, the ground shatters beneath your feet, and you plunge into darkness. In the void, a single eye opens, ancient and unfathomable. A voice fills your mind, soft but resonant: "Balance frays. Flames hunger. Vines strangle. Choose the path that preserves, or all shall wither. Seek the ember beneath the stone, the truth beneath the vine. Delay, and the harvest shall be ash."
You awaken with a start, the scent of smoke and earth lingering in your nostrils.
Having all experienced the same vision, you decide to recreate some of the images. To start, you note the symbols and decide to place them at the next transport circle you see. Chamae is also quick to note the tree symbol from the dream and notes that the area grows cold, and any nearby plants wilt on one side and flourish on the other—mirroring the tree’s dual nature. There’s a whispered message from who you think is Vel’Enar:
"Roots reach deep. Seek the ember’s cradle beneath ash and stone." Additionally, you get a flash of the cult’s next target - Lowri Greenway’s child. 
To mimic the choice, Gilbert holds a vine in one hand and a lit candle in the other, closing the fist on the vine to choose the ember. His hand glows with warmth, granting Advantage on Insight checks against deception for 24 hours. However, the fire seems to flicker around him, and cult members sense his presence more easily. With detect thoughts still active, he noticed a distinct focus of some people’s thoughts around him. Going downstairs to investigate, he saw a paladin with a big sword walk in and immediately clock him. Quickly dashing upstairs to inform the party, you break the window and climb down with Kalamari, Dawn, and Lera in tow.
