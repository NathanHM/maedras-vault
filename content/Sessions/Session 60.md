- The party continue speaking with [[Gary]] on the nature of the stolen time from their transport circles. [[Uta'ah]] points out that the math suggests that the party’s current impact on the world cannot be the only factor determining the amount of time lost when using a transport circle. Gary agrees, and the party ask him to determine what this other determining factor is, and how it can possibly be mitigated.
- Meanwhile, [[Cyrena]] and [[Gilbert]] convince [[Father Chard]] to run for Father of the Vine, and help him announce his candidacy with a musical number, placing the focus on his platform as a leader for all in the vine.
- Back in the wizard’s tower, [[Max]] grows tired of the mathematical discourse, and wanders off on their own to investigate the tower. Travelling up one floor, they find a blackboard with Vath’iran writings that the scholars had been unsuccessfully attempting to translate. Travelling up another floor, they find the wizards attemtping to create their own transport circle. A shadowed shape seems stuck in the circle, unable to exit.
- As Gary goes off to research for the party, [[Chamae]] and Uta’ah speak with another attendant. Uta’ah reveals to her that the Tayans are investigating a Vath’iran ruin, and the party are hoping the wizards could send [[Jameson Ludwig]] to investigate and report back to them.
- Uta’ah asks if the attendant has any free spell scrolls she could give the party. Deciding she hates her job and is going to quit anyway, she hands over spell scrolls for Teleport, Illusory Dragon, and Meteor Swarm.
- Uta’ah leaves the tower as Chamae is called on by Max to aid in getting the shadowed creature out of the transport circle. On the way up she observes the Vath’iran writings. One reads:
	Under moonlight glow,
	Awaiting virgin snow,
	I wait, I cry, I dissolve
- This points towards either Zerath, a supposedly haunted city in [[Tayan]], or Verath, a snowy area in [[Rulif]]. Supposedly people there are overcome with grief and throw themselves off a glacier.
- Other snippets of translations:
	- Divine flame powering first dawn
	- Bound for purity
	- Consuming creation
	- The twilight that dared bind the sun
	- You fear the dark but the light burns brighter than your fear ever could
- Joining Max upstairs, the two manage to free the shadowed creature from the transport circle, a raven that joins Max as they admonish the wizards for their poor work.
- The party reunites back in the tavern, where they decide to head towards the palace before the talks wrap up and the summit concludes.
