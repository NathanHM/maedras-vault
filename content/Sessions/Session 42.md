- The party contemplate [[Tayan]]'s guild culture. The nation is run by the council of guilds, composed of 16 of the most powerful guilds, with an elected head. [[Lord Votrin Deephelm]] of the trade guild is the current head of the council.
- The party debate taking their burgeoning travelling tavern business and climbing the guild system, but decide to hold those efforts off to a later date.
- They also discuss [[Tayan#Outsiders and the Tarnished|the Tarnished]], people of Tayan who have broken laws and have been relegated to a lesser class. They have limited rights and are identified by tattoos on their hands. The Tarnished are banished from guilds, though [[Uta'ah]] notes that they are welcomed within his [[Master Fizziwinkle's Travelling Wonders|circus]]. Fear of becoming tarnished keeps the people of Tayan in line, but also creates an environment that breeds feelings of rebellion and unrest.
