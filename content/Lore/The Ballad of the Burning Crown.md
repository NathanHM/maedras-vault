🎶 The Ballad of the Burning Crown

Verse I – The Crown Forged
Upon the anvil of fire and stone,
The smiths of old set a king on the throne.
In gold and flame, the crown was bound,
A kingdom’s fate in its circlet found.

Chorus
🔥 O Burning Crown, hold fast the flame,
Through war, through ash, through endless name.
From smoke to sky, from ash to ground,
All kneel before the Burning Crown. 🔥

Verse II – The Ashen Court
The court stood proud, the banners high,
But shadow stirred where sparks could lie.
A whispered oath, a pyre’s breath,
The Crown would burn to conquer death.

Chorus
🔥 O Burning Crown, hold fast the flame,
Through war, through ash, through endless name.
From smoke to sky, from ash to ground,
All kneel before the Burning Crown. 🔥

Verse III – The King’s Fall
The king was ash, the throne was bare,
The Crown still burned in empty air.
Those who seek its ember’s light,
Will trade the day to lose the night.

Final Chorus (Drawn out, ominous)
🔥 O Burning Crown, hold fast the flame,
Through war, through ash, through endless name.
From smoke to sky, from ash to ground,
All kneel before the Burning Crown…
…All kneel before the Burning Crown. 🔥
