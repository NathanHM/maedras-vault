
Bears a resemblance to [[The Ballad of the Burning Crown]]

> 🌑 _Verse 1_  
> In fields where night-lilies dare not bloom,  
> And roots curl ‘neath the ash-blown tomb,  
> There danced a child with fire for breath,  
> Who laughed at chains and whispered death.

> 🌘 _Verse 2_  
> A cradle lies in forest deep,  
> Where silence sings and branches weep.  
> The oldest oath, now cracked and worn,  
> Shall bleed again when flame is born.

> 🌕 _Chorus_  
> So spin, O wheel, of fire and vine,  
> The ancient heart beats down the line.  
> When circles blaze and smoke is crowned,  
> The soul once lost shall rise unbound.

> 🌒 _Verse 3_  
> Three masks walk where none should tread,  
> One walks for gold, one walks for dread,  
> The third walks on with guilt in hand—  
> The ash-child knows and makes its stand.

> 🌑 _Verse 4_  
> A pyre lit in garden grand,  
> With hollow kings on borrowed land.  
> The blood of kin, the laugh of flame,  
> The stone remembers all their names.

> 🌕 _Final Chorus_  
> So drink and dance, ye noble fools,  
> While servants sharpen sacred tools.  
> The blaze is near, the spark’s been fed—  
> The heart once cracked will burn instead.
