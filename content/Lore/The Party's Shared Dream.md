- You awaken in a field of **silver grass** beneath a sky split into **day and night**, the sun and moon hanging opposite each other  
- The air is still, and every sound feels distant, as if you’re underwater  
- Ahead stands a **vast tree** with obsidian-black bark, its branches twisting upward like skeletal fingers  
  - Half of its leaves are **vibrant gold**, while the other half are **ashen and burned**  
  - **Embers drift** from the charred branches, carried by a wind you cannot feel  
- At the tree’s base lies a **stone circle** carved with shifting runes, pulsing faintly  
- **Four tokens** float above it, each bearing the crest of a different nation:
  - A **silver wolf** for *Rulif*  
  - A **red blade** for *Qavrud*  
  - A **golden spiral** for *Alithi*  
  - A **black flame** for *Tayan*  
- One by one, the tokens fall onto the circle  
  - When the **black flame token** lands, the runes ignite with fire  
  - Cracks split the ground, spreading like veins of molten rock  
- The sky darkens, and from the horizon, a **wave of ash and fire** surges forward, swallowing the land  
- Out of the flame steps a **figure cloaked in shadows**, its face hidden  
  - In its hand: an **ember**, glowing with inviting warmth  
  - In the other: a **withered vine**, curling and reaching out like a grasping hand  
- The figure holds them out toward you—but before you can choose, the ground shatters beneath your feet  
- You plunge into **darkness**  
- In the void, a **single eye opens**, ancient and unfathomable  
- A voice fills your mind, soft but resonant:

  > *"Balance frays. Flames hunger. Vines strangle. Choose the path that preserves, or all shall wither. Seek the ember beneath the stone, the truth beneath the vine. Delay, and the harvest shall be ash."*

- **Chamae** quickly notes the **tree symbol** from the dream  
- The area grows **cold**, and nearby plants **wilt on one side** and **flourish on the other** — mirroring the tree’s dual nature  
- A **whispered message** is heard, likely from **Vel’Enar**:

  > *"Roots reach deep. Seek the ember’s cradle beneath ash and stone."*

- A sudden **flash of insight** reveals the cult’s **next target**: *Lowri Greenway’s child*
- You awaken with a start, the **scent of smoke and earth** lingering in your nostrils
