## Bio
- Evangeline is one of [[The Missing Children]], taken from [[Stormhaven]]
- She is currently still missing

---
## Involvement
- Her father asked the party to find and rescue her.
- Evangeline was the 12th child to go missing in 12 weeks in [[Rulif]].
- Evangaline was rescued from [[Stormhaven#The Mausoleum|the mausoleum]] unharmed. ([[Session 03]]) 
- Evangaline was trained Elvish under [[High Lady Dawn]] for funeral rites.
- Evangaline was later captured again by [[The Eternal Ember Cult]], alongside Dawn. ([[Session 10]])
- Though Dawn escaped, Evangaline has not been seen since.
