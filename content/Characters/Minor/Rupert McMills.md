## Bio
- Rupert McMills is a child from [[Stormhaven]].
- Rupert studied under [[High Lady Dusk]]. ([[Session 05]])
- Since being kidnapped, Rupert has lost the ability to speak and appears to have two minds within him.
---
## Involvement
- When the party spoke with Rupert, he could not speak and had to communicate via poorly written notes. ([[Session 07 08]])
- Rupert was gone for hours from his perspective but weeks from others, suggesting time may have been lost in [[Transport Circles]]. This could suggest Rupert is [[Divine Touch|Divine Touched]].
- The party later were able to identify the cult barracks in [[Lucanius]] as the location Rupert was taken. ([[Session 72]])
