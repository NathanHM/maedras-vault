## Bio
- Lera aspires to become a pianist
- She is either in her late teens or early twenties
- Lera has an older brother, Lawrence Vane, who has a higher position within the cult
- She was once the familiar of [[Kalamari]], cursed by [[Nuloran]] to live an immortal half-life until an insane octopus deems her free.
- Now she willingly serves as his translator.

---
## Involvement
- The party first encountered Lera when they infiltrated a [[The Eternal Ember Cult|cult]] recruitment drive in the [[Kilsa#Green Soldier|Green Soldier]]. ([[Session 17 18]])
- Uta'ah convinced her to pursue her dreams as a musician instead of joining the cult.
- However, speaking loudly and without care, he also brought Lera into danger as the cult tried to kill her along with the rest of the party.
- Unable to leave her alone and in danger, Uta'ah sent her to meet with [[High Lady Dawn]] and she tagged along with the party until she became Kalamari's familiar when the party was travelling to [[Lucanius]]. ([[Session 30]])
- Lera revealed to the party that she considered her new state of existence to be torturous. ([[Session 52]])
- After the party confronted Kalamari, she was freed from his service, but decided to willingly serve as a translator.
