## Bio
- Gary is a scholar working out of [[Lucanius]]
- He seems to experience time non-linearly, observing past Gary, present Gary, future Gary and all alternate timeline Gary’s perspectives at once.
- Gary is a peer of [[Jameson Ludwig]]
---
## Involvement
- Gary met with [[Max]], [[Chamae]] and [[Uta'ah]] in the wizard’s tower of Lucanius when they were seeking information on the nature of [[Transport Circles]]
- He revealed to the party that the increased loss of time is a consequence of their relative importance to the ongoing events dictating the state of the world
- The circles harvest the potential timelines that could have been created in the period the group spend travelling through them
- After Uta’ah countered that there must be some other factor dictating the loss of time beyond their own importance, Gary went to research what that could be, and how the party might counter it
