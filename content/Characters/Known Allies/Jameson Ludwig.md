## Bio
- A scholar studying [[Transport Circles]]
- A fanatic at heart, Jameson is well educated but willing to make leaps of logic
- Jameson fears [[Maedra Lurith]]
- He believes [[Chamae#Chamae's Constructs|Manny]] to be a living [[Vath'ira|Vath'iran]] 
- Though he considers himself an expert in the transport circles, he does not hold a high position within the scholars of Luconius

---
## Involvement
- The party first meet Jameson at the party prior to the peace summit. ([[Session 42]])
- He is studying symbols on the ground in the courtyard, which he believes to be part of an inactive transport circle.
- He helps teach [[Chamae]] how to read Vath'iran
- The party speak to Jameson again the next day. Speaking to Chamae through Manny, he is shocked to learn that she managed to change a symbol on the transport circle, something he has never heard of being successfully done before. Misunderstanding the situation, this leads him to believe that Manny is Vath'iran. ([[Session 47]])
- He informs them of the standard amount of time lost when using transport circles, speaks of rumours that the Fey were involved in the collapse of Vath'ira, and the possibility to travel forward and back in time with the circles
