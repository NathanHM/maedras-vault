## Bio
- Siobhan is a white dragon 
- Her full name is Shiverwatcher
- She is capable of shifting into a human form with some draconic features.
- The [[Vineyard]] owned by [[Halric Dain]] produces her favourite wine
- She and her sisters hunt in [[Lucanius#West Districts|The White Swan Coffee Shop]]
- She takes a particular interest in [[Uta'ah]]
- Siobhan has a sister, Sarah

---
## Involvement
- The party encounter Shiverwatcher after arriving in the [[Derelict Vineyard]]. In an attempt to scare off wolves, Uta'ah pisses to convince them a dragon is nearby and has marked the territory. The scent attracts Siobhan, convincing her a potential mate may be found. ([[Session 23]])
- Uta'ah is quickly taken with Siobhan, though the rest of the party is less willing to treat with her. They eventually agree to give her [[Bilbo Frankenfert]], claiming him to be a dragon expert, in turn for ferrying them to the vineyard where [[Juliana]] is being kept.
- There Siobhan aids the party in their attack against [[The Eternal Ember Cult]]. ([[Session 24 25 26]])
- They hand the three Julianas off to Siobhan, asking her to take care of Juliana in turn for letting her keep the other two.
- The party reconvene with Siobhan in the White Swan Coffee House, where she and Uta'ah step out for some private time. ([[Session 40]])
