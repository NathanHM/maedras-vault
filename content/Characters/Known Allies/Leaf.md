## Bio
- Leaf was a dancer working for the [[Kilsa#Pink Lotus|Pink Lotus]]
- Leaf was an adventurer prior to being contracted for the Pink Lotus
- He was bound by a magical contract to work for the Lotus
- Now freed from his contract, Leaf intends to return to her adventuring life
- His party includes [[Rose]] and [[Flora]].
- He is a rogue by nature.
- Since being freed of the contract, Leaf does not speak

---
## Involvement
- The party debated taking a job that would have saw them escort Leaf and other dancers from [[Kilsa]] to [[Lucanius]]
- The party later stole the contract binding him to the Pink Lotus
- [[Chamae]] dissolved the magic enforcing the contract.
- Upon being freed from the contract, she and her party resolved to investigate the [[Ashenhold]].
