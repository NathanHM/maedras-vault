## Bio
- Juliana is [[Gilbert]]'s mother
- Juliana has taken many lovers, including but not limited to [[Maedra Lurith]], the goddess [[Thalana]], and potentially one of the Thal twins.
- Juliana was blessed by Thalana, allowing her to use a [[Flame Orb]] without loss of her eyes
- Juliana seduced a pair of Changelings and convinced them to act as decoys when she was kidnapped by [[The Eternal Ember Cult]]

---
## Involvement
- The party first hear from Juliana when she attempts to send a letter to Gilbert. She has grown concerned about him since he left Bard college and has gone looking for him. ([[Session 15]]) 
- The mail was intercepted by the cult, and by the time it is received, Juliana was captured by the cult, and a threatening message was left for Gilbert signed from his mysterious father
- When the party found Juliana, she was accompanied by two identical copies. The group attempted to discern which version was the real one, but ultimately uncertain, they left all three in the safekeeping of [[Siobhan]]. ([[Session 24 25 26]])
- The party reunited with Juliana at [[Lucanius#Notable Buildings & Locations|The White Swan Coffee House]] with Siobhan. There she traumatises Gilbert by speaking openly about her sexual history, and reveals three candidates that could have been his father. ([[Session 39]])
	- A barbarian human, described as being good with his hips.
	- A roguish elf, said to be very talented with rope.
	- An Elf Juliana remembers for his diamond nipple and pearl penis piercings.
- She further traumatises Gilbert when she takes a Bard upstairs, and he hears noises that distinctly remind him of his favourite childhood lullaby. ([[Session 40]])
- After the fight at the peace talks, she is found in bed with [[Vaela]], unaware of her past relationship with Gilbert.
- She comforts the adventurers after they were freed from their contracts with [[Lord Tom Macavoy]].
