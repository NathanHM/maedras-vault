## Bio
- Rose is a dancer working for the [[Kilsa#Pink Lotus|Pink Lotus]]
- Rose was an adventurer prior to being contracted for the Pink Lotus
- She was bound by a magical contract to work for the Lotus
- Now freed from her contract, Rose intends to return to her adventuring life
- Her party consists of herself, [[Flora]], and the mute [[Leaf]].
- She is a barbarian by nature.

---
## Involvement
- The party first met Rose when she gave Gilbert a "massage". ([[Session 13]])
- The party debated taking a job that would have saw them escort Rose and other dancers to [[Lucanius]].
- The party later stole the contract binding her to the Pink Lotus.
- Chamae dissolved the magic enforcing her contract.
- Upon being freed from the contract, she and the party interrogated and killed [[Lord Tom Macavoy]].
- She then resolved to investigate the [[Ashenhold]] with her fellow adenturers.
