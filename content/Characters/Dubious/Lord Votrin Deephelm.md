## Bio
- Armed with the bravery and strength of five lions
- Votrin is the elected leader of the council of guilds, making him the leader of [[Tayan]]
- Votrin largely speaks in the form of limited time offers
- Has disagreements with [[Rellis Thorne]]

---
## Involvement
- The party first encounter Votrin at the party prior to the peace summit. ([[Session 41]])
- When talking to him during the summit, Votrin reveals that Tayan scholars have recently uncovered [[Vath'ira|Vath'iran]] writings. ([[Session 48]])
- The party also learn that [[Rellis Thorne]] is working with [[Merinth Tharrows|The Man]] on[[The Caravan Raids]]. ([[Session 51]])
