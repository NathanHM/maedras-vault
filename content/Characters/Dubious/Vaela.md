## Bio
- Vaela attended Bard college with [[Gilbert]]
- She went by the name Primrose in her time there
- She decided to move to [[Qavrud]] after meeting priests of [[Worship of Virelya|Virelya]]
- Is an aide to but has not fucked [[Lord High Priest Ferox Thal]]
- Gilbert claims they had a bad breakup and were toxic on both sides
- Vaela’s best friend from Bard college is international pop sensation [[Layla Swift]]

---
## Involvement
- The party first hear of Vaela in a [[Session 30|story]] from Gilbert on the way to [[Lucanius]]
- Outside of Ferox's room at [[The Peace Summit]], the party hear moans that Gilbert recognises
- Gilbert is shocked to [[Session 47|see]] Vaela arrive with Ferox at the summit
- As the [[Session 49 50|talks break into violence]], Gilbert makes an uncouth comment
- In retort, she attempts to cast suggestion on him and after failing summons fire elementals
- Gilbert manages to cast suggestion on her instead, telling her to protect him
- When [[The Emberheart]] attempts to kill everyone in the room, Vaela prays to [[Virelya]] and is able to stop him
- In [[Session 51|the aftermath]], the party talk to Vaela and find she seems to know nothing of Ferox' cult involvement. She is a true believer in Virelya, and moved to Qavrud some years ago
- She claims to have met [[Kaedric Thal]] once when she first arrived in Qavrud
