## Bio
- Ferox is the high priest and leader of Qavrud.
- Ferox has a twin brother, [[Kaedric Thal]].
- Ferox is one of the prime suspects to be [[Cyrena]] and [[Gilbert]]'s [[Diamond Nips and a Pearl Tip - The Mystery of Cyrena and Gilbert's Father|Father]].
- Ferox is also the religious leader of [[The Eternal Ember Cult]]. ([[Session 29]])
- He may also be the vassal of [[Virelya]]. ([[Session 46]])
- For the past 6 years, Ferox has been possessed by [[The Emberheart]]. ([[Session 51]])
- [[Vaela]] is a key aide to Ferox. ([[Session 47]])
- Ferox may be under threat from [[Dareth Vonn|usurpers]] in Qavrud. ([[Session 41]])

## Involvement
- The party first hear of Ferox when Gilbert receives a note, addressed from his father, that resembles Ferox's handwriting. ([[Session 15]])
- They next hear about Ferox when interrogating a cult priest in [[Kilsa]], who mentions him and his twin brother, Kaedric. ([[Session 17 18]])
- Ferox is confirmed to be involved in the cult when the party interrogate [[Vladimir Vladishkov|Vlad]]. ([[Session 29]])
- The party hear that Ferox's party is occupying the Merry Elf in [[Lucanius]] for the peace summit. ([[Session 32]])
- The party find a set of [[#Notes on Lord Ferox Thal|notes]] on Ferox in the [[Lucanius#Notable Buildings & Locations|Forgotten Archive]]. ([[Session 33 34]])
- The party finally meet Ferox at a party prior to the peace summit, which he is attending with [[High Lady Dusk]]. ([[Session 40]])
- [[Uta'ah]] accidentally lets slip to [[Dareth Vonn]] that Ferox may be acting against the tenets of the [[Worship of Virelya]]. ([[Session 41]])
- Listening outside of Ferox's room, the party hears intimate noises from Ferox's room with a female voice that Gilbert recognises.
- Ferox arrives at the talks with Vaela, a rival of Gilbert's from bard college. ([[Session 47]])
- At the summit, [[Chamae]] realises that Ferox is possessed by the Emberheart. ([[Session 48]])
- As the peace talks break into violence, the Emberheart attempts to annihilate the party but is prevented by Virelya possessing Vaela. ([[Session 49 50]])
- The party interrogate Ferox and come to the conclusion that he was possessed by the Emberheart six years ago. ([[Session 51]])
- They have [[Maedra Lurith|Maedra]] brand Ferox with glyphs to prevent possession. ([[Session 52]])

## Notes on Lord Ferox Thal
A scholar recently researching Lord Ferox Thal has uncovered several intriguing details:
1. **Unusual Alliances**  
   In the past year, Lord Thal has been seen working closely with merchants from Tayan and emissaries from Rulif, suggesting he’s fostering unexpected diplomatic ties. While this may be a move to stabilize Qavrud’s position, some suspect he’s securing rare resources or influence for future leverage.
2. **Interest in Ancient Lore**  
   Rumours suggest Thal has funded several expeditions to sites linked to the ancient civilization that once built the transport circles. This is unusual given traditionalist Qavrudian views on magic. Some speculate he's seeking forgotten knowledge or artifacts that could shift the balance of power.
3. **Coded Correspondence**  
   Recent intercepted messages between Qavrud agents mention a "burning heart beneath the waves" — a possible reference to the Sunken Sanctum or the Emberheart? Whether Thal is seeking to control or destroy this power remains unknown. This is merely a rumour as these notes have not been seen to be verified.
4. **A Shift in Policy**  
   Scholars note that Thal's stance toward religious orders has shifted dramatically. Once indifferent, he’s recently encouraged stronger ties with the Church of Vel'Enar. Some suspect he may be aligning with certain reverend mothers or leveraging the church’s influence for political gain. Again, unusual given Qavrud’s isolationist religious practices, though they have begun sending out missionaries.
5. **Internal Strife**  
   Qavrud’s court is reportedly divided. While Thal maintains dominance, whispers suggest that key figures within his own council question his growing fascination with dangerous relics and foreign alliances. This could be mere rumours as the power held by Lord Thal as the high religious figure is always under a tight watch.

A scholar examining these clues may conclude that Thal is playing a long and calculated game — one that could reshape the balance of power in the region.
