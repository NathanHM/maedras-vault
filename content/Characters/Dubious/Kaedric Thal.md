## Bio
- Kaedric Thal is the twin brother of [[Lord High Priest Ferox Thal]]
- He is one of the prime suspects to be  [[Diamond Nips and a Pearl Tip - The Mystery of Cyrena and Gilbert's Father|Cyrena and Gilbert's father]]
- He may be the vassal of [[Virelya]]
- He once wandered the nations
- Kaedric supposedly died in a ritual sacrifice 6 years ago, before returning

## Involvement
- The party first learn of Kaedric from [[The Eternal Ember Cult]] priest in [[Kilsa]]. ([[Session 17 18]])
- The party hear conflicting information about Kaedric. Some claim he and Ferox are twins, whilst others say they are one and the same.
- [[Vaela]] says she met Kaedric at the docks when she arrived Qavrud. ([[Session 51]])
