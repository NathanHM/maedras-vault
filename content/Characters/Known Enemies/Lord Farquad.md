## Bio
- Lord Farquad is a [[The Eternal Ember Cult|cult]] ally working out of [[Stormhaven]]
- Lord Farquad's sister is [[Merinth Tharrows]], though Farquad hates her due to [[Rulif]]'s matriarchal culture placing her first in line for their inheritance
- As a result, Farquad likes to pretend she is dead
- Farquad is drinking buddies with [[Vladimir Vladishkov]], [[Bilbo Frankenfert]] and [[Fizziwinkle]]
- Farquad is said to be newly allergic to [[Lavender]]
- Lord Farquad fought in [[The War]]
- Farquad rides his horse, Starry, daily
