## Bio
- Father Ophius is a head priest with [[The Eternal Vine]]
- He is an ally of [[The Eternal Ember Cult]]

---
## Involvement
- The party met Ophius when investigating a series of grave robbings.
- They freed [[Barbarastreisand]] and the other nuns, all of whom were in indentured servitude.
- After the death of Father Tiberius, Ophius was the presumptive next leader of the Eternal Vine.
- The party trashed his reputation to prevent his ascension.
