## Bio
- Merinth Tharrows is The Man
- She is the sister of [[Lord Farquad]]
- Due to [[Rulif]]'s matriarchal society, she inherited the bulk of the family's assets over him
- Merinth is one of the three heads of [[The Eternal Ember Cult]], leading their spy network
- She is orchestrating [[The Caravan Raids]] with the aid of [[Rellis Thorne]]
---
## Involvement
- The party first met Merinth Tharrows at the masquerade ball, though knew nothing of significance about her at the time
- They later [[Session 03|encountered]] bandits involved with the theft of [[Talis Greenway]]' amulet who were hired by "TM" 
- Farquad was [[Session 09|supposedly]] threatened to support [[The Sashed Paladins]] by "TM", whom he speculated to be [[Lord Tom|Tom Macavoy]]
- The party [[Session 17 18|learn]] The Man is an important cult figure from a priest in [[Kilsa]]
- The Man's identity is finally [[Session 28|confirmed]] as Merinth Tharrows by [[Bilbo Frankenfert]]
- [[Vladimir Vladishkov|Vlad]] confirms she is one of the three heads of the cult, leading their spy network
- [[Gilbert]] [[Session 43|encounters]] a figure he recognises as Merinth Tharrows at the party prior to [[The Peace Summit]]
- She is knocked unconscious by [[Uta'ah]], but then seems to turn into a different person. Suspecting that Tharrows was somehow possessing this woman, Uta''ah sends her off to the care of [[High Lady Dawn]], but she never arrives
- The party [[Session 52|discover]] that the head mage under [[Lord Regent Titus]] is a woman named Trina Macbeth. They assume this to be another identity of The Man, though her description does not match that of Merinth Tharrows.
- They discover that Macbeth has disappeared during [[The Peace Summit]], though a [[Session 53|discovered]] logbook confirms she was infiltrating the court
- [[Session 54|Investigating the sewers]], they find the body of Macbeth. Further on, the party meet a group of [[Myconids]] who communicate telepathically. When prompted with images of Trina Macbeth and Merinth Tharrows, the Myconids respond with the same sense of fear.
- The party [[Session 55|encounter]] a [[Fey]] woman in the sewers who appears similar, but not identical, to Tharrows.  She reveals that she has been hired to challenge the party, and animates a group of dead ogres to fight the party before vanishing.
