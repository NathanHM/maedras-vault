## Bio
- Fizziwinkle is an ally of [[The Eternal Ember Cult]]
- He is the owner of [[Master Fizziwinkle's Travelling Wonders]], some say for tax avoidance reasons
- Fizziwinkle is drinking buddies with [[Vladimir Vladishkov|Vlad]], [[Bilbo Frankenfert]] and [[Lord Farquad]]

---
## Involvement
- The party learn of Fizziwinkle upon meeting [[Uta'ah]], as he searches for Fizziwinkle to find his mentor, Miss Haley.
- The party learn Fizziwinkle is involved in [[The Missing Children|the child abductions]] from [[Vladimir Vladishkov]]. ([[Session 20 21]])
- Uta'ah finds out that Fizziwinkle was last known to be living in the wealth district of [[Lucanius]].
