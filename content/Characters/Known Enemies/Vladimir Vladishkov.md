## Bio
- Vlad was originally from the USSR. How he came to be in this world is unknown
- Vlad was drinking buddies with [[Lord Farquad]], [[Bilbo Frankenfert]] and [[Fizziwinkle]]
- He was the leader of [[The Eternal Ember Cult#The Ember Hand|The Ember Hand]]
- Vlad was a true believer in [[The Emberheart]]
- Vlad loves a twink
- Vlad is currently separated from his head
- The party is now in possession of Vlad's sword

## Involvement
- Vlad was [[Session 07 08|seemingly]] involved in the [[The Missing Children|abduction]] of [[Evangaline]]
- Farquad was [[Session 09|found]] to be funding Vlad and [[The Sashed Paladins]]
- [[Gilbert]] accidentally [[Session 19|summons]] Vlad when he carries out a cult ritual
- [[Max]] [[Session 20 21|talks]] to Vlad in disguise as a twink paladin, discovering more about Vlad and his goals
- The party [[Session 24 25 26|fight]] Vlad in the [[Vineyard]] to rescue [[Juliana]]
- The party [[Session 29|interrogate]] Vlad, eventually killing him after he becomes possessed by The Emberheart
