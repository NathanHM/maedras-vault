## Bio
- Vlad was originally from the USSR. How he came to be in this world is unknown
- Vlad was drinking buddies with [[Lord Farquad]], [[Bilbo Frankenfert]] and [[Fizziwinkle]]
- He was the leader of [[The Eternal Ember Cult#The Ember Hand|The Ember Hand]]
- Vlad was a true believer in [[The Emberheart]]
- Vlad loves a twink
- Vlad is currently separated from his head
- The party is now in possession of Vlad's sword

## Involvement
- Vlad was seemingly involved in the [[The Missing Children|abduction]] of [[Evangaline]]. ([[Session 07 08]])
- Farquad was found to be funding Vlad and the Ember Hand. ([[Session 09]])
- [[Gilbert]] accidentally summons Vlad when he carries out a cult ritual. ([[Session 19]])
- [[Max]] talks to Vlad in disguise as a twink paladin, discovering more about Vlad and his goals. ([[Session 20 21]])
- The party fight Vlad in the [[Vineyard]] to rescue [[Juliana]]. ([[Session 24 25 26]])
- The party interrogate Vlad, eventually killing him after he becomes possessed by The Emberheart. ([[Session 29]])
