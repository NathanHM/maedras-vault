## Bio
- Rellis Torne is the head of [[Tayan#The Black Blades|The Black Blades]]
- She is the head of the Tayan Mercenary Guild
- Rellis is an ally of [[The Eternal Ember Cult]], fuelling [[The Caravan Raids]]
