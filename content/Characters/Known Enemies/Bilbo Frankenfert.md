## Bio
- Bilbo is the manager of the [[Kilsa]] transport hub
- He is a relatively low level member of [[The Eternal Ember Cult]]
- He is a drinking buddy of [[Vladimir Vladishkov|Vlad]], [[Fizziwinkle]] and [[Lord Farquad]]

---
## Involvement
- The party first meet Bilbo after emerging from a [[Transport Circles|circle]] beneath his transport hub in Kilsa. ([[Session 13]])
- He provides the party accommodation in the [[Kilsa#Purple Tulip|Purple Tulip]] in exchange for a transport token
- The party learn he is close with [[Vladimir Vladishkov|Vlad]]. ([[Session 20 21]])
- On their way out of Kilsa, Bilbo attempts to poison [[Max]] and [[Cyrena]] on the instruction of [[The Flamekeeper]]. ([[Session 22]])
- A [[Flame Orb]] is discovered to be hidden in Bilbo's office. ([[Session 23]])
- The party promise to give Bilbo to [[Siobhan]] in turn for her aid.
- The party interrogate Bilbo for information on the cult, successfully convincing him that [[Gilbert]] is [[The Emberheart]]. ([[Session 28]])
- I think Bilbo has been tied up in Lifty since then, not sure we ever actually gave him to Siobhan.
