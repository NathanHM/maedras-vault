## Bio
- Bilbo is the manager of the [[Kilsa]] transport hub
- He is a relatively low level member of [[The Eternal Ember Cult]]
- He is a drinking buddy of [[Vladimir Vladishkov|Vlad]], [[Fizziwinkle]] and [[Lord Farquad]]

---
## Involvement
- The party first meet Bilbo after [[Session 13|emerging]] from a [[Transport Circles|circle]] beneath his transport hub in Kilsa
- He provides the party accommodation in the [[Kilsa#Purple Tulip|Purple Tulip]] in exchange for a transport token
- The party [[Session 20 21|learn]] he is close with [[Vladimir Vladishkov|Vlad]]
- On their way out of Kilsa, Bilbo [[Session 22|attempts]] to poison [[Max]] and [[Cyrena]] on the instruction of [[The Flamekeeper]]
- A [[Flame Orb]] is [[Session 23|discovered]] to be hidden in Bilbo's office
- The party promise to give Bilbo to [[Siobhan]] in turn for her aid
- The party [[Session 28|interrogate]] Bilbo for information on the cult, successfully convincing him that [[Gilbert]] is [[The Emberheart]]
- I think Bilbo has been tied up in Lifty since then, not sure we ever actually gave him to Siobhan
