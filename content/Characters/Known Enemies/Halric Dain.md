## Bio
- Halric Dain is an advisor to [[Lord Regent Titus]], but hopes to take his place as thee ruler of [[Alithi]]
- He is a high ranking member of [[The Eternal Ember Cult]]
- Dain is pushing for modernisation, with increased militarization and industralisation in Alithi, moving away from the traditional values of [[The Eternal Vine]]
- Dain is the owner of the [[Vineyard]] outside [[Lucanius]] where the cult were keeping [[Juliana]]
