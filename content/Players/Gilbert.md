## Bio
- Gilbert was raised by his mother, [[Juliana]]
- He never knew his [[Diamond Nips and a Pearl Tip - The Mystery of Cyrena and Gilbert's Father|father]]
- He attended Bard college before leaving with his mentor, Melody "Thistle" Higginsbottom
- He had a rival in bard college, [[Vaela|Primrose]]
- Gilbert struggles with a gambling addiction (See [[Session 02]])

---
## Story
- Gilbert is one of the founding party members
- He [[Session 15|received]] a diamond containing a spell of Mass Healing from [[Father Ellric]]
- He found his mother had come looking for him, only for her to be taken by the cult as a hostage
- He was able to rescue his mother with the party's aid
- He discovered that he had a half-sister, [[Cyrena]]
- He suspects his father to be Lord Ferox, or his brother Kaedric
- Gilbert has repeatedly impersonated the Emberheart
