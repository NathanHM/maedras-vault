## Bio
- Chamae is a Fairy
- She suffers from amnesia, unable to remember her life from before about three years ago
- Chamae also deals with anxiety, preferring to stay within Lifty when she can, and facing social situations through her mannequin, Manny
- Chamae has been [[Divine Touch|Divine Touched]] by [[Ceylira]]

---
## Story
- Chamae was one of the founding members of the party
- When the party [[Session 15|received ]] mail, she got a job offer from her old friend Quarkus, with a hidden message: “You're not safe, meet us in [[Stormhaven]] when you can”
- Chamae learned to fly after a heart warming montage with [[Gilbert]].

---
## Chamae's Constructs
- Chamae has a few assistants she has built to aid her on her adventures.
- Manny is the most prominent, her Steel Defender, a mannequin that accompanies her and handles a lot of her face to face interactions.
- Lifty, a large spider-like mech with a tent Chamae lives in, only leaving when she must. 
- Shiny, a dragonfly-esque construct that can be used to stealthily spy and preform minor actions
- Lin Lampwell Miranda, a friendly Fae lamp that Chamae found in the Forgotten Archive
- Shifty is the latest addition, Chamae's Wizard familiar, a small silver lizard she can look through the eyes of. Shifty can be summoned in other forms when needed.
