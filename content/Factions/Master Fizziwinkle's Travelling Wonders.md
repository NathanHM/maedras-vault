Master Fizziwinkle's Travelling Wonders is a Tayan circus. Some have called it the the greatest show in the world. Others have called it a tax avoidance scheme. A few have even claimed "I thought Hugh Jackman was in this." Who's to say which is true?

---
## Members
- [[Fizziwinkle]] is in fact not a member of the circus. Though he is the owner, and the name and face of the circus, he has never in fact travelled with the circle. Members of the circus claim that he owns the circus for tax avoidance purposes.
- [[Uta'ah]] is the circus acrobat, the son of I'owa and O'hi'o, trained by Miss Haley. Raised in the circus, he has little experience of the outside world.
- Miss Haley is the Ringmaster and General Manager of the circus. Once the resident acrobat, now she is the de facto leader and head of the circus family. She recently departed the circus on a trip to meet Fizziwinkle for reasons unknown.
- I'owa is Uta'ah's mother, and the circus magician. Gifted with no natural magical capabilities, she is reliant on slight of hand and performance to sell all her tricks.
- O'hi'o is Uta'ah's father. A white Dragonborn like his son, he is a natural ice breather. This makes his circus show of fire breathing all the more impressive. Though, perhaps less so than if he were not Dragonborn at all.
- Brog is an Orcish animal tamer, who performs with his Owlbear, Thena. Occasionally Thena has killed a few audience members, but honestly it was probably their fault.
- A Halfling strong-woman named Bathilda the Big. Rumours say she may even have an incredible strength score of 13.
- Dame Esmerelda, the Elvish divinator. She is prophesised to save the world. So she says.
