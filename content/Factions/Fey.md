- The Fey are a largely unknown entity within the continent. Living in the plane of the Feywild, their politics and lives seem largely divorced from the rest of the world. 
- The Cult would like to bring the Feywild into the material plane to cause death and chaos, powering the Emberheart.
---
## Known Fey
- [[Max]] is a Changeling originally from the Feywild
- [[Chamae]] is also Fey, but not knowing her own origins makes it hard to determine what links she has to the Feywild and its people.
- [[High Lady Dawn]] is a Changeling like Max, so presumably also has some ancestry from the Feywild.
- [[Talis Greenway]] originally comes from the Feywild, meaning her son [[Dayne Greenway]] also has Fey ancestry
- [[Maedra Lurith]] is the most prominent Fey the party has met, an Arch Fey of the Unseelie. She appears to be on unsteady ground with her people, as they set out a group to prevent her from gaining access to the Forgotten Archive in [[Lucanius]]
- Sheila was the leader of the group that attempted to stop Maedra, and seemed to have some connection to her, with Maedra remarking on her with some fondness.
- The [[Myconids]] appear to originate from the Feywild, though appear to be several generations removed from that time.
- [[Merinth Tharrows|The Man]] may be Fey. After chasing Trina Macbeth, a suspected alternate identity of Tharrows, into the sewers beneath Lucanius, the group found a Fey who resembled Tharrows. This potentially may also suggest her brother, [[Lord Farquad]] is Fey.
- [[Iotrareno]] was a Fey the party encountered in the Church of Vel'Enar in Lucanius. Initially assumed to be the Emberheart due to her golden eyes, she was watching the dimmed flame and anyone else who was interested. She reacted to mentions of Maedra, and Max banished her to the Feywild.
- In a recently uncovered memory, Chamae encountered an old woman suspected to be involved with her lost memories. Max believes this woman to be [[Verenestra]], the daughter of the queen of the summer court.

## Relationship with [[Vath'ira]]
- There are [[Session 47|rumours]] that the Fey played a part in the downfall of Vath'ira. They warned the people that their [[Transport Circles]] would upset the natural balance of magic. Whether the fall of Vath'ira was their warnings being proven accurate, or was the Fey taking matters into their own hands is unclear.
